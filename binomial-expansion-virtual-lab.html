Binomial Expansion

Build expansions, inspect coefficients, and understand the general term step by step.

1. The binomial theorem

For a non-negative integer n, the expansion of (x + y)n contains n + 1 terms. The coefficient of each term comes from a combination.

(x + y)n = Σr=0n ⁿCᵣ xn−ryr

The general term is Tr+1 = ⁿCᵣ xn−ryr. Remember: the index r starts at 0, while the term number starts at 1.

3. Worked examples

  1. (a + b)³: coefficients 1, 3, 3, 1 give a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³.
  2. (x − 2)⁴: use y = −2: x⁴ − 8x³ + 24x² − 32x + 16.
  3. Coefficient of x² in (x + 3)⁴: choose r = 2, so ⁽⁴⁾C₂ × x² × 3² = 6 × 9 = 54.
  4. General term: in (x + y)ⁿ, Tr+1 = ⁿCᵣxn−ryr. Substitute the required r carefully.

4. Pascal's triangle

Each interior number is the sum of the two numbers immediately above it. These rows provide the binomial coefficients.
