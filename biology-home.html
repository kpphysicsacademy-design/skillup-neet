02Structural Organisation in Animals and Plants

Plants: Morphology and modifications; tissues; anatomy and functions of root, stem, leaf, inflorescence (cymose and racemose), flower, fruit and seed; relevant practical work; families Malvaceae, Cruciferae, Leguminosae, Compositae and Graminae.

Animals: Animal tissues; morphology, anatomy and functions of digestive, circulatory, respiratory, nervous and reproductive systems of an insect and frog; brief account.

03Cell Structure and Function

Cell: Cell theory and cell as the basic unit of life; prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells; plant and animal cells; cell envelope, cell membrane and cell wall.

Organelles: Structure and function of endomembrane system — endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi bodies, lysosomes and vacuoles; mitochondria, ribosomes, plastids and microbodies; cytoskeleton, cilia, flagella and centrioles; nucleus, nuclear membrane, chromatin and nucleolus.

Biomolecules: Proteins, carbohydrates, lipids and nucleic acids — structure and function; enzymes — types, properties, enzyme action, classification and nomenclature.

Cell division: Cell cycle, mitosis, meiosis and their significance.

04Plant Physiology

Photosynthesis: Autotrophic nutrition; site of photosynthesis; pigments (elementary idea); photochemical and biosynthetic phases; cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation; chemiosmotic hypothesis; photorespiration; C3 and C4 pathways; factors affecting photosynthesis.

Respiration: Gas exchange; cellular respiration — glycolysis, anaerobic fermentation, TCA cycle and aerobic electron transport system; energy relations and ATP generation; amphibolic pathways; respiratory quotient.

Plant growth and development: Seed germination; phases and rate of plant growth; conditions for growth; differentiation, dedifferentiation and redifferentiation; developmental sequence; growth regulators — auxin, gibberellin, cytokinin, ethylene and ABA.

05Human Physiology

Breathing and respiration: Respiratory organs in animals (recall); human respiratory system; mechanism and regulation of breathing; exchange and transport of gases; respiratory volumes; asthma, emphysema and occupational respiratory disorders.

Body fluids and circulation: Blood composition, blood groups and coagulation; lymph and its functions; human heart and blood vessels; cardiac cycle, cardiac output, ECG, double circulation; regulation of cardiac activity; hypertension, coronary artery disease, angina pectoris and heart failure.

Excretion: Ammonotelism, ureotelism and uricotelism; human excretory system; urine formation; osmoregulation; kidney regulation including renin-angiotensin, atrial natriuretic factor and ADH; other organs in excretion; uraemia, renal failure, renal calculi, nephritis, dialysis and artificial kidney.

Locomotion: Ciliary, flagellar and muscular movement; skeletal muscle, contractile proteins and muscle contraction; skeletal system and joints; myasthenia gravis, tetany, muscular dystrophy, arthritis, osteoporosis and gout.

Neural control: Neuron and nerves; central, peripheral and visceral nervous systems; generation and conduction of nerve impulse.

Chemical coordination: Endocrine glands and hormones; hypothalamus, pituitary, pineal, thyroid, parathyroid, adrenal, pancreas and gonads; elementary mechanism of hormone action; hormones as messengers and regulators; common hypo- and hyperactivity disorders.

06Reproduction

Sexual reproduction in flowering plants: Flower structure; development of male and female gametophytes; pollination, types, agencies and examples; outbreeding devices; pollen-pistil interaction; double fertilization; post-fertilization events including endosperm, embryo, seed and fruit; apomixis, parthenocarpy and polyembryony; significance of seed and fruit formation.

Human reproduction: Male and female reproductive systems; microscopic anatomy of testis and ovary; spermatogenesis and oogenesis; menstrual cycle; fertilisation; embryo development to blastocyst; implantation; pregnancy and placenta (elementary idea); parturition and lactation (elementary idea).

Reproductive health: Need for reproductive health; prevention of STDs; birth control and contraception; MTP; amniocentesis; infertility and assisted reproductive technologies — IVF, ZIFT and GIFT (elementary idea).

07Genetics and Evolution

Heredity and variation: Mendelian inheritance; incomplete dominance; co-dominance; multiple alleles and blood groups; pleiotropy; elementary polygenic inheritance; chromosome theory; chromosomes and genes.

Sex determination and disorders: Humans, birds and honey bee; linkage and crossing over; sex-linked inheritance including haemophilia and colour blindness; Mendelian disorder thalassemia; chromosomal disorders including Down syndrome, Turner syndrome and Klinefelter syndrome.

Molecular basis: Search for genetic material and DNA as genetic material; DNA/RNA structure; DNA packaging; replication; central dogma; transcription; genetic code; translation; gene expression and regulation including Lac operon; genome and Human Genome Project; DNA fingerprinting; protein biosynthesis.

Evolution: Origin of life; evidence from palaeontology, comparative anatomy, embryology and molecular evidence; Darwin; modern synthetic theory; mutation and recombination; natural selection and its types; gene flow and genetic drift; Hardy-Weinberg principle; adaptive radiation; human evolution.

08Biology and Human Welfare

Health and disease: Pathogens and parasites causing human diseases including malaria, filariasis, ascariasis, typhoid, pneumonia, common cold, amoebiasis, ringworm, dengue and chikungunya; basic immunology and vaccines; cancer; HIV and AIDS; adolescence; drug and alcohol abuse; tobacco abuse.

Microbes in human welfare: Household food processing; industrial production; sewage treatment; energy generation; biocontrol agents and biofertilisers.

09Biotechnology and Its Applications

Principles and process: Genetic engineering and recombinant DNA technology.

Applications in health and agriculture: Human insulin and vaccine production; gene therapy; genetically modified organisms including Bt crops; transgenic animals; biosafety issues; biopiracy and patents.

10Ecology and Environment

Organisms and environment: Population interactions — mutualism, competition, predation and parasitism; population attributes — growth, birth rate, death rate and age distribution.

Ecosystem: Patterns and components; productivity and decomposition; energy flow; pyramids of number, biomass and energy.

Biodiversity and conservation: Concept, patterns and importance of biodiversity; loss of biodiversity; conservation; hotspots; endangered organisms; extinction; Red Data Book; biosphere reserves; national parks and sanctuaries; sacred groves.
