What Is Living?

One-minute revision of the key features of living organisms.

Life = organisation + metabolism + response
🎯 Practise Quiz📘 Topic Cheat Sheet

1. What makes an organism living?

Living organisms are self-replicating, evolving and self-regulating systems that interact with and respond to their environment.

Cellular organisation

Living organisms have an organised cellular structure.

Metabolism

All chemical reactions occurring in a living system.

Consciousness

Ability to sense and respond to stimuli.

Growth & reproduction

Common life features, but not universal tests on their own.

2. Growth & reproduction

Growth

Living growth occurs from within through cell division or enlargement.

Reproduction

Produces new individuals and maintains continuity of a species.

Remember: Crystals grow by external deposition. Mules and sterile worker bees do not reproduce. So growth and reproduction alone do not define life.

3. Metabolism

The sum total of all biochemical reactions in a living organism.

Anabolism · builds

Simple substances → complex substances. Example: protein synthesis.

Catabolism · breaks

Complex substances → simpler substances. Example: cellular respiration.

Metabolism = Anabolism + Catabolism

4. Consciousness: response to stimuli

Living organisms sense changes in their surroundings and respond to them.

StimulusExample
LightShoot bends towards light
TouchMimosa pudica leaves fold
GravityRoots grow towards gravity

5. Exam quick recap

1

Crystal growth is not life — it occurs by external deposition.

2

Reproduction is not universal — mule and sterile worker bee are examples.

3

Metabolism is a defining feature — it includes all biochemical reactions.

4

Plants are conscious in the biological sense — they sense and respond to stimuli.

Memory line: Cells · Metabolism · Response. Growth and reproduction are useful clues, not absolute tests for every individual.
