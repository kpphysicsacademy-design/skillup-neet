Atom, Molecule, Element & Compound

Atom: the smallest particle of an element that retains the chemical identity of that element.

Molecule: a group of two or more atoms chemically bonded together and capable of independent existence.

Element: a pure substance made of only one kind of atom.

Compound: a pure substance formed when two or more different elements combine chemically in a fixed proportion.

🔗 MoleculeH₂, O₂, H₂O
🧩 ElementHydrogen, oxygen, iron
