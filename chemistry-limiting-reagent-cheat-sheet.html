⚖️ Limiting Reagent

Smart revision card • Remember the ratio, find the smallest

ONE-LINE DEFINITION

What is it?

The reactant consumed first; it limits the maximum amount of product formed.

GOLDEN FORMULA

Compare each reactant

\[\frac{\text{Available moles}}{\text{Balanced coefficient}}\]

Smallest ratio = Limiting reagent

First balance the equation. Convert mass to moles using \(n=\frac{m}{M}\).
4-STEP SHORTCUT
1Balance
2Find moles
3Divide by coefficient
4Choose smallest
SOLVED IN 10 SECONDS

\(2H_2+O_2\rightarrow2H_2O\)

Given: 2 mol \(H_2\) and 2 mol \(O_2\).

\(H_2: 2/2=1\)

\(O_2: 2/1=2\)

Smallest = 1 → H₂ is limiting.

Water formed = 2 mol; oxygen left = 1 mol.

EXAM MEMORY
LimitingUsed up completely
ExcessLeft unreacted
Theoretical yieldMaximum product
Exact ratioNo reactant left over

Yield % = (Actual yield ÷ Theoretical yield) × 100
