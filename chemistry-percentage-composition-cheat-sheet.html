Percentage Composition

The percentage by mass of each element present in a compound. Learn the formula, calculate it instantly and check your result.

1. Key formula

For an element X in a compound:

\\[\\%X=\\frac{\\text{Mass of X in the compound}}{\\text{Total mass of compound}}\\times100\\]

From a chemical formula, use:

\\[\\%X=\\frac{n_X\\times A_r(X)}{M_r(\\text{compound})}\\times100\\]

The percentages of all elements in a pure compound add up to approximately 100% (small differences may occur due to rounding).

4. Exam shortcut

  1. Find the molar mass of the compound.
  2. Calculate the mass contributed by the required element(s).
  3. Divide the element's mass contribution by the compound's molar mass.
  4. Multiply by 100 to get percentage.

Example: H₂O

\\[M_r=2(1.008)+15.999=18.015\\;\\text{g mol}^{-1}\\]
\\[\\%H=\\frac{2(1.008)}{18.015}\\times100=11.19\\%\\]
\\[\\%O=\\frac{15.999}{18.015}\\times100=88.81\\%\\]
