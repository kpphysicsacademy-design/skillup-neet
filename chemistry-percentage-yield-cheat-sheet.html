📈 Percentage Yield

Smart revision card • Compare actual product with the maximum possible

ONE-LINE DEFINITION

What is percentage yield?

The percentage of the theoretical product that is actually obtained in an experiment.

GOLDEN FORMULA

Calculate the yield

\[\%\text{ yield}=\frac{\text{Actual yield}}{\text{Theoretical yield}}\times100\]

Actual = experimental • Theoretical = maximum predicted

Rearrange when needed: Actual yield = (% yield ÷ 100) × Theoretical yield.
4-STEP SHORTCUT
1Find limiting reagent
2Calculate theoretical yield
3Note actual yield
4Divide × 100
SOLVED EXAMPLE

Find percentage yield

Theoretical yield = 40 g; actual yield = 30 g.

\[\%\text{ yield}=\frac{30}{40}\times100\]

\[=0.75\times100=75\%\]

Answer: 75% yield.
EXAM MEMORY
Theoretical yieldMaximum product from limiting reagent
Actual yieldProduct obtained experimentally
100% yieldActual = theoretical
Above 100%Check purity, moisture or calculation

Remember: Product loss, incomplete reaction and side reactions can lower actual yield.
