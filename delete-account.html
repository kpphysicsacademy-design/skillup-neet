Privacy and account controls

Request Account Deletion

Use this page to learn how to request deletion of your SkillUp account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the official SkillUp app listing on Google Play and use the developer contact email shown under App support.
  2. Send a message with the subject SkillUp account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your SkillUp account and, if available, your account username so the request can be verified.
  4. Do not include your password or other sensitive information.

The developer may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

What happens after a request

The developer will review the request and, after verifying ownership, delete the account and associated personal data that the service controls, subject to applicable legal, security, and fraud-prevention retention requirements. Any data that must be retained will be limited to what is required and kept only for the applicable retention period.

Deleting the app from your device does not itself delete your account or associated data.

Data that may be affected

Depending on the features you used, deletion may include account/profile information and saved learning activity or quiz progress associated with the account. Information held by third-party services may be subject to their own processes and applicable law.

Privacy policy

Read the SkillUp Privacy Policy for additional information about privacy and data requests.

Publisher action required: Ensure the Google Play listing has a monitored developer contact email and that requests sent there are actually processed. This page describes the request route; it does not automatically delete an account.
