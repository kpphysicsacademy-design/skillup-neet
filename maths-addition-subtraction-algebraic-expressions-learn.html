Addition and Subtraction of Algebraic Expressions

Understand like terms, add expressions and subtract without sign mistakes.

1. What is an algebraic expression?

An algebraic expression combines numbers, variables and operations. In \(3x+5\), 3 is the coefficient, x is the variable and 5 is the constant.

2. Like terms

Like terms have the same variables raised to the same powers. Their coefficients may differ.

\(4x+7x=(4+7)x=11x\)

But \(3x\) and \(3x^2\) are unlike terms and cannot be combined.

3. Add expressions

\((3x+2)+(5x+4)\)
Remove brackets: \(3x+2+5x+4\)
Group like terms: \((3x+5x)+(2+4)\)
Add: \(8x+6\)

4. Subtract expressions

When a bracket has a minus sign before it, change the sign of every term inside.

\((7x+5)-(2x+3)\)
Change signs: \(7x+5-2x-3\)
Group: \((7x-2x)+(5-3)\)
Answer: \(5x+2\)

5. Remember

  • Combine only like terms.
  • Keep unlike terms separate.
  • For subtraction, change every sign inside the second bracket.

6. Check your understanding

Simplify \((4a+3)+(2a+5)\).

\((4a+2a)+(3+5)=6a+8\)
