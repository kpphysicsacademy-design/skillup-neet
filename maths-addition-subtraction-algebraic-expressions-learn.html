Addition & Subtraction of Algebraic Expressions

Learn one simple rule: group like terms, then add or subtract their coefficients.

THE GOLDEN RULE
3x + 5x = 8x

Same variable and same power? Combine the numbers in front. Keep the variable part unchanged.

1. Spot like terms

LIKE TERMS ✓
4x and 7x
Same variable and power.
UNLIKE TERMS ✕
4x and 4x²
Different powers. Do not combine.
Remember: x means 1x. So 5x − x = 5x − 1x = 4x.

2. Addition — keep the signs

Remove brackets, group like terms, and add.

Example
(3x + 2) + (5x + 4)
1
Remove brackets: 3x + 2 + 5x + 4
2
Group: (3x + 5x) + (2 + 4)
3
8x + 6

3. Subtraction — change every sign

A minus sign before a bracket changes the sign of every term inside that bracket.

Example
(7x + 5) − (2x + 3)
1
Change signs: 7x + 5 − 2x − 3
2
Group: (7x − 2x) + (5 − 3)
3
5x + 2
Watch out: −(2x + 3) becomes −2x − 3, not −2x + 3.

4. Try one more example

Simplify:
(4a + 3) + (2a + 5)
Group variable terms and constants:
(4a + 2a) + (3 + 5)
6a + 8
