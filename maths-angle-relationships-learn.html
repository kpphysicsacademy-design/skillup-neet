Angle Relationships

A quick lesson to recognize angle pairs, choose the right rule, and calculate unknown angles.

✏️ Quiz📐 Simulator
01 • COMPLEMENTARY ANGLES

Complementary angles

Two angles are complementary when their measures add to a right angle.

a + b = 90°
Example: complement of 34° = 90° − 34° = 56°.
02 • SUPPLEMENTARY ANGLES

Supplementary angles

Two angles are supplementary when their measures add to a straight angle. They need not be adjacent.

a + b = 180°
Example: supplement of 127° = 180° − 127° = 53°.
03 • LINEAR PAIR

Adjacent angles on a straight line

A linear pair consists of adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line.

a + b = 180°
Example: if one angle is 68°, the other is 112°.
04 • INTERSECTING LINES

Vertically opposite angles

When two straight lines intersect, opposite angles are equal. Adjacent angles form a linear pair.

a = c   and   b = d
Example: if one angle is 74°, its vertically opposite angle is also 74°; each adjacent angle is 106°.
05 • ALGEBRA

Find the unknown

If two angles form a linear pair and are (3x + 10)° and (2x + 20)°:

3x + 10 + 2x + 20 = 180
5x + 30 = 180
x = 30. The angles are 100° and 80°.

Quick revision checklist

• Complementary → total 90°
• Supplementary → total 180°
• Linear pair → adjacent and total 180°
• Vertically opposite → equal
• Around a point → total 360°
• Always form the equation before solving for x.
