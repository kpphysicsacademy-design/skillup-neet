Angle Sum Property of a Triangle

Brief Learn · Class 7 foundation · Understand, calculate and check

1. The key property

For every plane triangle, the sum of its three interior angles is always 180°.

∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°

The property works for acute, right, obtuse, isosceles and equilateral triangles.

2. Find a missing angle

Example: Two angles are 52° and 68°. Find the third angle.

∠C = 180° − (52° + 68°) = 60°

Method: Add the known angles, then subtract their total from 180°.

3. Algebra-based questions

If the angles are x°, 2x° and 3x°:

x + 2x + 3x = 180°

6x = 180°, so x = 30°. The angles are 30°, 60° and 90°.

4. Useful triangle facts

  • Equilateral: all three angles are 60°.
  • Isosceles: the two base angles are equal.
  • Right triangle: the other two angles add up to 90°.
  • Exterior angle: an exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.

5. Check your understanding

A triangle has angles 35° and 85°. Its third angle is 60°, because 180° − 35° − 85° = 60°.

Common mistake: do not use 360°. The 360° total applies to a full turn or the interior angles of a quadrilateral, not a triangle.
