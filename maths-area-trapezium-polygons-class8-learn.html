Area of Trapezium & Polygons

Understand the formulas with short steps and easy examples.

🧮 Try Smart Simulator📝 30-Question Quiz

1. Area of a trapezium

A trapezium has one pair of parallel sides. Its height is the perpendicular distance between those parallel sides.

Area = ½ × (a + b) × h

a and b = parallel sides; h = perpendicular height.

Example
Parallel sides = 6 cm and 10 cm; height = 4 cm.
Add parallel sides: 6 + 10 = 16
Multiply by height: 16 × 4 = 64
Take half: 64 ÷ 2 = 32 cm²

2. Find a missing height

h = (2 × Area) ÷ (a + b)
Example
Area = 72 cm²; parallel sides = 10 cm and 14 cm.
h = (2 × 72) ÷ (10 + 14)
h = 144 ÷ 24 = 6 cm

3. Know your polygons

3 sidesTriangle
4 sidesQuadrilateral
5 sidesPentagon
6 sidesHexagon
8 sidesOctagon
10 sidesDecagon

A regular polygon has all sides equal and all interior angles equal.

4. Interior-angle sum

Sum = (n − 2) × 180°

n = number of sides.

Example: Pentagon
(5 − 2) × 180° = 3 × 180° = 540°.

5. Regular polygon angles

Each interior angle = Sum ÷ n
Each exterior angle = 360° ÷ n
Example: Regular hexagon
Interior-angle sum = 720°; each interior angle = 720° ÷ 6 = 120°. Each exterior angle = 360° ÷ 6 = 60°.
