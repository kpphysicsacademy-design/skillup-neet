Basic Constructions Simulator

Explore accurate ruler-and-compass methods. Change measurements, follow the construction stages, and see how arcs locate the required points.

📝 Practice Basic Constructions Quiz

1. Construct and bisect a line segment

Set a segment length. The midpoint divides it into two equal parts.

2. Perpendicular bisector using compass arcs

Draw equal-radius arcs from both endpoints. Their two intersections determine a line that cuts the segment at 90° and halfway.

3. Bisect an angle

An angle bisector divides an angle into two equal angles. Adjust the original angle and explore the equal halves.

4. Perpendicular through a point on a line

Choose a point on a straight line. Equal-distance marks on either side help locate the compass-arc intersection and construct a 90° line.

5. Copy a line segment

Measure a source segment, transfer the same compass opening, and mark an equal length from a new starting point.
