Chord Properties

Brief Class 9 lesson: key theorems, diagrams and one worked example.

📝 Practise Quiz🧪 Simulate

1. Chord and diameter

A chord is a line segment joining two points on a circle. A diameter is the longest chord because it passes through the centre.

2. Perpendicular from centre

The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord.
OM ⟂ AB ⇒ AM = MB

Converse: the line joining the centre to the midpoint of a chord is perpendicular to the chord.

3. Equal chords

Equal chords of the same circle are equidistant from the centre. Chords equidistant from the centre are equal.
AB = CD ⇔ d₁ = d₂

In the same circle, a chord closer to the centre is longer.

4. Equal central angles

Equal chords of the same circle subtend equal angles at the centre; equal central angles subtend equal chords.
AB = CD ⇔ ∠AOB = ∠COD

Use this property when a problem gives central angles instead of chord lengths.

\n

5. Chord length formula

The radius, perpendicular distance and half-chord form a right triangle.

r² = d² + (L/2)²
L = 2√(r² − d²)

Example: If r = 13 cm and d = 5 cm:

L = 2√(169 − 25) = 2 × 12 = 24 cm

Remember

  • Diameter is the longest chord.
  • Perpendicular from centre bisects a chord.
  • Equal chords are equidistant from centre.
  • Use Pythagoras for chord-length problems.
