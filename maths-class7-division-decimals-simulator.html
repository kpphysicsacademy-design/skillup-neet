Learning tip
Change one value at a time and observe how the quotient changes.
Activity 01 · Place value

Divide by 10, 100 or 1,000

Explore how division changes the value of each digit.

Explore
Dividing by 10, 100 or 1,000 moves the decimal point left by 1, 2 or 3 places.
Activity 02 · Calculate

Decimal divided by a whole number

Divide a decimal dividend by a positive whole-number divisor.

Calculate
Use place value to position the decimal point in the quotient. Check by multiplying quotient × divisor.
Activity 03 · Make divisor whole

Divide by a decimal

Multiply both numbers by the same power of 10 to make the divisor a whole number.

Strategy
Whatever you multiply the divisor by, multiply the dividend by the same amount. The quotient stays unchanged.
Activity 04 · Estimate and verify

Estimate first, then check

Compare the quotient with a nearby whole-number estimate and verify by multiplication.

Reason
Use compatible numbers for an estimate. Multiplying the exact quotient by the divisor should return the dividend.
Activity 05 · Apply

Divide quantities in real life

Find how many equal groups can be made from a total quantity.

Apply
Try a rope cut into equal lengths, juice poured into glasses, or distance travelled per litre.
