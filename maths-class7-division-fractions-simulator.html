Division of Fractions

Explore each example, change the numbers and read the short explanation after every activity.

📝 Practice the 30-question quiz

1. Reciprocal explorer

Divide one fraction by another.

Brief explanation: Keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, and flip the second fraction. The flipped fraction is called its reciprocal.

2. Fraction-bar model

Find how many unit-fraction pieces fit into a fraction.

Brief explanation: Division asks “how many groups fit?” For example, 3/4 ÷ 1/8 asks how many eighths are contained in three-fourths.

3. Simplify before multiplying

Cancel common factors after flipping the divisor.

Brief explanation: A common factor in a numerator and denominator can be divided out first. This keeps the numbers smaller without changing the answer.

4. Mixed-number division

Divide mixed numbers by converting them first.

Brief explanation: Change each mixed number into an improper fraction: whole number × denominator + numerator. Then use the reciprocal method.

5. Sharing and measuring

Share a fractional quantity equally.

Brief explanation: To share a fraction among a whole number of people, divide by that number. This is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

6. Quick challenge

Solve the problem and enter the fraction in simplest form.

Brief explanation: Apply keep–change–flip, multiply across, and reduce the final fraction.
