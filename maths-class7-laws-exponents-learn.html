Laws of Exponents

Use the laws to multiply, divide and simplify powers with the same base.

1. Product of powers

When multiplying powers with the same base, add the exponents.

aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

Example: 2³ × 2² = 2⁵.

2. Quotient of powers

When dividing powers with the same non-zero base, subtract the exponents.

aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ

Example: 5⁴ ÷ 5² = 5².

3. Power of a power

When a power is raised to another power, multiply the exponents.

(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ

Example: (3²)³ = 3⁶.

4. Power of a product

(ab)ⁿ = aⁿbⁿ

Example: (2 × 3)² = 2² × 3².

5. Zero exponent

a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0)

Any non-zero number raised to the power zero equals 1.

6. Negative exponent

a⁻ⁿ = 1 ÷ aⁿ (a ≠ 0)

A negative exponent means take the reciprocal.

Quick reminder

Multiply same bases → add powers. Divide same bases → subtract powers. Power raised to a power → multiply powers.
