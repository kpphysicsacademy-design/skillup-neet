Workshop 03 · Simplify

Make the numbers smaller first

Cancel common factors diagonally before multiplying. The value stays exactly the same.

Number strategy
A common factor can be divided out of one numerator and the opposite denominator. This is equivalent to dividing the whole product by the same factor.
