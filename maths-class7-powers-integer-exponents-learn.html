CLASS 7 · EXPONENTS

Powers with Integer Exponents

Understand the base, exponent and value of a power, including positive, zero and negative integer exponents.

1. Base and exponent

In aⁿ, a is the base and n is the exponent. The exponent tells how many times the base is used as a factor when n is a positive integer.

3⁴ = 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81

2. Positive integer exponents

aⁿ = a × a × … × a (n factors)

Example: 5³ = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

3. Zero exponent

a⁰ = 1, when a ≠ 0

Every non-zero number raised to zero equals one.

4. Negative integer exponents

a⁻ⁿ = 1 / aⁿ, when a ≠ 0

A negative exponent represents the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power.

2⁻³

= 1 / 2³ = 1 / 8

5. Negative bases

(−2)³ = −8

An odd number of negative factors gives a negative product.

(−2)⁴ = 16

An even number of negative factors gives a positive product.

6. Parentheses matter

(−3)² = 9

The negative sign is part of the base.

−3² = −9

Without parentheses, the exponent applies to 3 first.
