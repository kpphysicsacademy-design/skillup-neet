Problem-solving steps
Identify the quantities, choose an operation, calculate with units, then check whether the answer makes sense.
Activity 01 · Shopping & money

Calculate a shopping bill

Find the cost of several items, add them and apply a discount.

Money
Model: (number of items × price per item) + extra cost − discount. Money is written to two decimal places.
Activity 02 · Recipe fractions

Scale a recipe

Multiply the amount per batch by the number of batches.

Fractions
Example: 1/2 cup per batch × 3 batches = 3/2 cups = 1 1/2 cups.
Activity 03 · Sharing & measurement

Find equal groups or pieces

Divide a total quantity by the amount in each group.

Division
Example: 5.6 kg shared into 0.4 kg portions gives 14 portions.
Activity 04 · Distance & speed

Combine journeys and find speed

Add distances from two trips or calculate average speed.

Measurement
Total distance = first journey + second journey. Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.
Activity 05 · Multi-step problems

Solve a tank or field problem

First find a fraction of the whole, then add or subtract the remaining quantity.

Reason
Example: a tank is 3/4 full and holds 80 L. It contains 60 L; after 18 L is used, 42 L remains.
