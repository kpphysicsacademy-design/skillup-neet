Collecting & Organising Data

Turn a set of survey answers into a tally table, frequency table and bar chart.

📝 Practise the 30-question quiz

1. Enter survey responses

Type one response per line, or separate responses with commas. Repeated answers are counted automatically.

2. Tally and frequency table

A tally group represents five responses. Frequency is the total count for each category.

3. Read the bar chart

Compare the bar lengths to identify the most and least frequent categories.
