4. Integer comparison rules

Positive vs negative: every positive integer is greater than every negative integer.
Two negative integers: the integer closer to zero is greater. For example, −2 > −6.
Zero: zero is greater than every negative integer and less than every positive integer.
Number line: a value farther right is always greater than a value farther left.
