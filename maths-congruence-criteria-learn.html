Congruence Criteria

Brief Learn · Class 9 · Compare corresponding sides and angles

1. What are congruent triangles?

Two triangles are congruent when they have exactly the same shape and size. One can be placed over the other so that all corresponding vertices and sides coincide.

△ABC ≅ △PQR means A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R

The order of the letters is important: it tells you which vertices correspond.

2. SSS — Side-Side-Side

If all three corresponding sides of two triangles are equal, the triangles are congruent.

AB = PQ, BC = QR, AC = PR

Use SSS when three matching side lengths are known.

3. SAS — Side-Angle-Side

If two corresponding sides and the angle included between those sides are equal, the triangles are congruent.

AB = PQ, AC = PR, ∠A = ∠P

The angle must be between the two given sides. A non-included angle gives SSA, which is not a general congruence rule.

4. ASA — Angle-Side-Angle

If two corresponding angles and the side included between them are equal, the triangles are congruent.

∠A = ∠P, AB = PQ, ∠B = ∠Q

The known side lies between the two known angles.

5. RHS — Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side

For two right-angled triangles, if their hypotenuses and one corresponding side (leg) are equal, the triangles are congruent.

∠B = ∠Q = 90°, AC = PR, AB = PQ

RHS applies only when both triangles are right-angled.

6. What is not enough?

  • AAA: equal corresponding angles establish similarity, not necessarily equal size.
  • SSA: two sides and a non-included angle do not guarantee congruence in general.
  • Equal perimeter: does not prove that corresponding sides and angles match.
Remember: for SAS, check the angle is included; for ASA, check the side is included; for RHS, confirm both triangles are right-angled.

7. CPCT

After proving two triangles congruent, use CPCT — Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles — to conclude that their matching sides and angles are equal.

△ABC ≅ △PQR ⇒ AB = PQ and ∠B = ∠Q

Proof-writing sequence

  1. Write the two triangles being compared.
  2. List the given equal sides and angles.
  3. Check that the information matches one valid criterion.
  4. State the congruence in correct vertex order.
  5. Use CPCT for any further equal sides or angles.
