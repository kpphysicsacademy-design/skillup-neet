Cube Roots by Prime Factorisation

Break a number into prime factors, group identical factors in threes, then take one factor from each group.

If N = a³ × b³ × c³, then ∛N = abc

1. What is a cube root?

The cube root of a number is the value which, when multiplied by itself three times, gives that number.

∛216 = 6 because 6 × 6 × 6 = 216

2. Prime factorisation method

  1. Write the number as a product of prime factors.
  2. Arrange equal prime factors together.
  3. Make groups of three identical factors.
  4. Take one factor from each complete group and multiply them.

For a perfect cube, every prime factor has an exponent that is a multiple of 3.

3. Worked example: ∛216

216 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 3

Make two groups: (2 × 2 × 2) and (3 × 3 × 3). Take one factor from each group.

∛216 = 2 × 3 = 6

4. Larger example: ∛1728

1728 = 2⁶ × 3³

There are two groups of three 2s and one group of three 3s.

∛1728 = 2 × 2 × 3 = 12

5. What if a factor is left over?

If a prime factor cannot be placed into a complete group of three, the number is not a perfect cube. For example:

72 = 2³ × 3²

The three 2s form one group, but the two 3s do not form a complete group. Therefore 72 is not a perfect cube.

6. Verify your answer

Cube the result you found. It should reproduce the original number.

6³ = 216 ✓

For negative integers, keep the negative sign: ∛(−216) = −6.
