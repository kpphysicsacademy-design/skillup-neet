Cyclic Quadrilaterals

Brief Class 9 lesson · Key theorems, diagram and worked examples.

📝 Practise Quiz🧪 Open Simulator

1. What is a cyclic quadrilateral?

A quadrilateral is cyclic when all four of its vertices lie on the circumference of one circle. Its sides are chords of that circle.

A, B, C and D all lie on the same circle, so ABCD is cyclic.

2. Opposite angles are supplementary

The sum of either pair of opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral is 180°.
∠A + ∠C = 180°
∠B + ∠D = 180°
Example: If ∠A = 72°, then ∠C = 180° − 72° = 108°.

3. Exterior angle property

An exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral is equal to the interior opposite angle.
Exterior angle at A = ∠C

Extend one side at a vertex. The exterior angle and the adjacent interior angle form a linear pair; use the opposite-angle theorem to show it equals the interior angle at the opposite vertex.

Example: If the interior opposite angle C is 104°, the exterior angle at A is also 104°.

4. How to identify a cyclic quadrilateral

Look for four points on one circle, or use a known angle condition. A quadrilateral with a pair of opposite angles adding to 180° is cyclic. Do not confuse adjacent angles with opposite angles.

Opposite pair only: 180°

5. Converse theorem

If a pair of opposite angles of a quadrilateral adds up to 180°, the quadrilateral is cyclic.
∠A + ∠C = 180° ⇒ ABCD is cyclic

This is a useful test when the question does not directly tell you that the four vertices lie on a circle.

6. Solve angle problems step by step

  1. Identify which angles are opposite.
  2. Write their sum as 180°.
  3. Form an equation if the angles contain x.
  4. Solve for x, then substitute to find the required angle.
Example: Opposite angles are (2x + 10)° and (3x + 20)°.
2x + 10 + 3x + 20 = 180
5x = 150, so x = 30°.
The angles are 70° and 110°.

Quick memory points

  • All four vertices lie on one circle.
  • Opposite angles add to 180°.
  • An exterior angle equals the opposite interior angle.
  • A supplementary opposite-angle pair proves cyclicity.
