Decimal Expansions

Identify a decimal pattern and connect it to rational or irrational numbers.

1. Terminating

Digits end after a finite number of places.

0.125 = 1/8

2. Recurring

A digit or fixed block repeats forever.

0.272727… = 3/11

3. Non-recurring

Digits continue forever without a repeating block.

√2 = 1.414213…

4. Rational or irrational?

Terminating and recurring decimals are rational. Non-terminating, non-recurring decimals are irrational.

π is irrational
Fast denominator rule
Reduce p/q first. Its decimal terminates exactly when the denominator has only prime factors 2 and/or 5. Example: 3/40 terminates; 2/7 repeats.
Convert a recurring decimal
Let x = 0.333…; then 10x = 3.333… . Subtract: 9x = 3, so x = 1/3.
