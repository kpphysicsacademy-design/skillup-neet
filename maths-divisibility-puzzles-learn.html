Divisibility Puzzles

Learn quick ways to check numbers. Then use the rules to find missing digits and solve puzzles.

🧩 Try Simulator✍️ Practise Quiz

1. What does divisible mean?

A number is divisible by another number when it divides exactly, with no remainder.

Example: 24 ÷ 6 = 4, remainder 0. So 24 is divisible by 6.

2. Easy divisibility rules

÷ 2Last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.
÷ 3Add the digits. The sum must divide by 3.
÷ 4Check the last two digits.
÷ 5Last digit is 0 or 5.
÷ 6It must pass both the 2 and 3 tests.
÷ 8Check the last three digits.
÷ 9Add the digits. The sum must divide by 9.
÷ 10Last digit is 0.
÷ 11Alternate adding and subtracting digits; the result is 0 or a multiple of 11.

3. How to solve a missing-digit puzzle

Find the digit that makes 5□2 divisible by 3.

Step 1: Add the known digits: 5 + 2 = 7.
Step 2: Try digits that make the total a multiple of 3.
Step 3: The possible digits are 2, 5 and 8 because the totals are 9, 12 and 15.

4. Divisible by two numbers

If a number must divide by both numbers, it must pass both tests.

For divisibility by 6: the number must be even and its digit sum must divide by 3.
For divisibility by 12: it must divide by 3 and by 4.

5. Number patterns and common multiples

Look at the pattern 8, 16, 24, 32, .... Each number is a multiple of 8. The first one also divisible by 9 is 72.

8 × 9 = 72
72 ÷ 8 = 9 and 72 ÷ 9 = 8.

6. Remember these steps

  1. Read which number the question asks you to divide by.
  2. Choose the correct divisibility rule.
  3. Try all possible missing digits.
  4. Check your answer by division.
  5. If asked for the smallest or greatest digit, compare all valid digits.

Watch out: For divisibility by 6, being even is not enough. The number must also be divisible by 3.
