EXPRESSION
24x³ ÷ 6x
SIMPLIFIED RESULT
4x²
Step 1: Divide coefficients: 24 ÷ 6 = 4.
Step 2: Subtract exponents: x³ ÷ x¹ = x².
Step 3: Result = 4x².

2. Remember the rule

xᵐ ÷ xⁿ = xᵐ⁻ⁿ

The bases must be the same and the divisor must not be zero. When dividing a polynomial by a monomial, divide every term separately.
