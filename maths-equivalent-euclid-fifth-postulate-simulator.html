Euclid’s Fifth Postulate Simulator

Adjust the two same-side interior angles and test the sum against 180°.

✏️ Quiz📘 Brief Learn
Same-side interior angle sum
180°
Parallel case: the interior angles are supplementary.
110° + 70° = 180°

Interpret the result

If the sum is below 180°, the lines meet on the side where that angle sum occurs. If it is exactly 180°, the lines are parallel. If it is above 180°, they meet on the opposite side, where the supplementary sum is below 180°.
