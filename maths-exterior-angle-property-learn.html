Exterior Angle Property of a Triangle

Brief Learn · Class 7 foundation · Understand, calculate and apply

1. The key property

When one side of a triangle is extended, the exterior angle formed equals the sum of the two opposite (remote) interior angles.

Exterior angle = ∠A + ∠B

The two remote interior angles are the interior angles that are not adjacent to the exterior angle.

2. Find an exterior angle

Example: The two remote interior angles are 35° and 75°.

Exterior angle = 35° + 75° = 110°

Add the two opposite interior angles to find the exterior angle.

3. Find a missing remote angle

Example: An exterior angle is 120°. One remote interior angle is 50°.

Missing angle = 120° − 50° = 70°

4. Link with a linear pair

The exterior angle and its adjacent interior angle lie on a straight line, so they add to 180°.

Exterior angle + adjacent interior angle = 180°

For example, if the exterior angle is 125°, its adjacent interior angle is 55°.

5. Algebra application

If the exterior angle is (3x + 10)° and the remote interior angles are 70° and 60°:

3x + 10 = 70 + 60
3x = 120
x = 40
Common mistake: do not add the adjacent interior angle to the two remote angles. The exterior angle equals only the sum of the two remote interior angles.
