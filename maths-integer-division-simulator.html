📝 Practice Integer Division Quiz

1. Quotient explorer

Change the dividend and divisor. Try all four sign combinations and zero.

2. Four sign rules

(+) ÷ (+) = +Same signs → positive
(−) ÷ (−) = +Same signs → positive
(+) ÷ (−) = −Different signs → negative
(−) ÷ (+) = −Different signs → negative

Division by zero is undefined. Zero divided by any non-zero integer is zero.

3. Equal-sharing model

Share a signed total equally among a chosen number of groups.

4. Division and multiplication connection

Use the inverse relationship: if a ÷ b = c, then c × b = a (where b is not zero).

5. Real-life signed sharing

Interpret a negative total shared equally, such as a debt or a downward change.
