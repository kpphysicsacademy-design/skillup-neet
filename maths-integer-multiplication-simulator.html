1. Sign-rule explorer

Change both factors and observe the sign and value of their product.

2. Understand the four sign combinations

A product is positive when the factors have the same sign and negative when their signs differ.

(+) × (+) = +Same signs → positive
(−) × (−) = +Same signs → positive
(+) × (−) = −Different signs → negative
(−) × (+) = −Different signs → negative

3. Repeated-addition model

For a positive multiplier, multiplication counts equal groups. A negative multiplier reverses the direction of the total change.

4. Product pattern explorer

Keep one factor fixed and multiply it by consecutive integers to discover how the products change.

5. Real-life repeated change

Model repeated gains or losses, rises or descents.
