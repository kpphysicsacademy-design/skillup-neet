Integer Subtraction Simulator

Understand subtraction of positive and negative integers. Visualize movement, rewrite subtraction as addition of the opposite, and solve real-life difference problems.

📝 Practice Integer Subtraction Quiz

1. Number-line explorer

Start at the first integer. Subtracting a positive moves left; subtracting a negative moves right.

2. Add-the-opposite counter model

Use a − b = a + (−b). Replace the number being subtracted with its opposite, then cancel positive-negative pairs.

3. Explore the subtraction rule

Change either integer and see how subtracting a negative differs from subtracting a positive.

4. Real-life difference explorer

Compare two signed values. The difference is first value minus second value.

5. Quick challenge
