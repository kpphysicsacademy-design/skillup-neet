Large Numbers in Daily Life

Learn how to read, compare and round large numbers. No quiz—just simple examples.

1. Where do we use large numbers?

We use large numbers to describe populations, money, distances and many everyday quantities.

1,25,000 — one lakh twenty-five thousand
₹8,50,000 — eight lakh fifty thousand rupees

2. Read a large number

5,08,24,019

Five crore eight lakh twenty-four thousand nineteen.

In the Indian system, place commas after the last three digits, then after every two digits.

3. Compare and round

Compare: 6,75,000 is greater than 6,57,000.

Compare digits from the left. The first place where the digits differ tells you which number is larger.

Round: 4,87,600 is about 4,88,000 to the nearest thousand.

Rounding gives a nearby number that is easier to use.

Remember

Read the number in groups, check each digit's place, then compare or round when needed.
