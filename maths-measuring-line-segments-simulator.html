Measuring Line Segments Lab

Practise ruler readings, measure segments from non-zero starts, convert units and combine lengths.

1. Interactive ruler

Drag the endpoints to measure a segment. The ruler shows centimetres and millimetres.

2. Unit converter

Convert between millimetres, centimetres and metres.

3. Compare segment lengths

Enter two lengths and choose their units to compare them in centimetres.

4. Joined segments challenge

Find the total length when segments are joined end to end without overlap.
