Midpoint Theorem · Brief Learn

A quick revision before you practise.

Theorem

The line segment joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and half its length.

If D and E are midpoints: DE ∥ BC and DE = ½ BC

Converse

A line drawn through the midpoint of one side of a triangle, parallel to a second side, bisects the third side.

Proof idea

Draw DE parallel to BC through midpoint D of AB. By parallel-line angle relationships, △ADE ∼ △ABC.

Therefore AD/AB = AE/AC = DE/BC = 1/2. Since AD = DB, E is also the midpoint of AC and DE is half of BC.

Remember

• Midpoint means two equal segments.

• The mid-segment is parallel to the third side.

• Its length is half the third side.

• The converse lets you prove a point is a midpoint.
