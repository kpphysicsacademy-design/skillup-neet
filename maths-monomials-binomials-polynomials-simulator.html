Polynomial Simulator

Change the numbers and see what happens. Three simple tools help you understand polynomials.

1 · EXPLORE

Identify the polynomial

Enter an expression in x. The simulator combines like terms, counts the terms and finds the degree.

\(4x^2-3x+7\)
Trinomial 3 terms · Degree 2
Simplified form: \(4x^2-3x+7\)
2 · ADD

Add two polynomials

Enter two expressions. Like powers are grouped and added together.

Sum \(4x^2-3x+3\)
\(3x^2+x^2=4x^2\)
\(2x-5x=-3x\)
\(-1+4=3\)
3 · EVALUATE

Find the value of \(P(x)\)

For this example, use \(P(x)=ax^2+bx+c\). Change a, b, c and x.

Calculated answer \(P(2)=3\)
\(P(2)=2(2)^2-3(2)+1\)
\(=8-6+1=3\)
