Multiplication of Algebraic Expressions

Learn four simple rules. Multiply terms, open brackets, expand two brackets and use special identities.

THE GOLDEN RULE
2x × 3x = 6x²

Multiply the numbers. For the same variable, add the powers: x × x = x².

1. Multiply terms

NUMBERS
3 × 4 = 12
Multiply coefficients.
VARIABLES
a² × a = a³
Add exponents: 2 + 1.
Example
5a × 2a²
= 10a³
Remember: x² × x³ = x⁵. Add powers only when the base is the same.

2. Multiply a term by a bracket

Multiply the outside term by every term inside the bracket.

Example
2a(3a + 5)
1
Multiply 2a × 3a = 6a².
2
Multiply 2a × 5 = 10a.
3
6a² + 10a

3. Multiply two brackets

Each term in the first bracket multiplies each term in the second bracket.

Example
(x + 2)(x + 3)
1
x × x = x²
2
x × 3 = 3x, and 2 × x = 2x
3
2 × 3 = 6
4
Combine like terms: x² + 3x + 2x + 6
x² + 5x + 6

4. Three useful identities

Square of a sum
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
Square of a difference
(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
Difference of squares
(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

5. Sign reminder

Same signs
+ × + = +
− × − = +
Different signs
+ × − = −
− × + = −
When expanding brackets, multiply the signs along with the numbers and variables.
