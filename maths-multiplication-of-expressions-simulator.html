Multiplication Simulator

Try multiplying a term by a bracket or multiplying two brackets. See every product and the simplified result.

1. Term × bracket

Example: \(2x(3x+4)\). Enter the outside term and the two inside terms.

Expanded result\(6x^2+8x\)
\(2x\times3x=6x^2\)
\(2x\times4=8x\)

2. Bracket × bracket

Example: \((x+2)(x+3)\). Enter two terms in each bracket.

Simplified result\(x^2+5x+6\)
\(x\cdot x+x\cdot3+2\cdot x+2\cdot3\)
\(=x^2+3x+2x+6\)
\(=x^2+5x+6\)
