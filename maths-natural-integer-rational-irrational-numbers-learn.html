🔢 Number Systems

Six number types. One simple guide. Learn the definition and recognise each type.

1

Natural numbers (N)

Counting numbers starting from 1.

1, 2, 3, 4, …
2

Whole numbers (W)

Natural numbers including zero.

0, 1, 2, 3, …
3

Integers (Z)

Negative whole numbers, zero and positive whole numbers.

…, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, …
4

Rational numbers (Q)

Can be written as p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

1/2 · −3/4 · 0.25 · 0.333…
5

Irrational numbers

Cannot be written as p/q. Their decimals never end and never repeat.

√2 · √3 · π
6

Real numbers (R)

All rational and irrational numbers together.

R = Rational ∪ Irrational

⚡ Remember these 2 rules

Number order: N ⊂ W ⊂ Z ⊂ Q ⊂ R

Decimal test: Terminates or repeats = Rational. Never ends and never repeats = Irrational.
