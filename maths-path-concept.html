Equivalent Rational Numbers

Learn the concept, explore it interactively, then complete the dedicated 30-question mastery quiz.

Step 1 • Learn the Concept

Equivalent rational numbers have the same value.

Multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

\(\frac{a}{b}=\frac{a\times k}{b\times k},\quad k\ne0,\ b\ne0\)

Example: \(\frac{2}{3}=\frac{4}{6}=\frac{6}{9}\).
