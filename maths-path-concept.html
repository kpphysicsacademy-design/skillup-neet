Equivalent Rational Numbers

Learn the idea first, explore it interactively, then complete a dedicated 30-question mastery quiz.

Step 1 • Learn the Concept

Rational numbers are numbers that can be written as p/q.

Here p and q are integers and q is not zero. Rational numbers include integers, fractions, terminating decimals, and repeating decimals.

\(\frac{p}{q},\quad p,q\in\mathbb{Z},\ q\ne0\)

Examples: \(\frac{3}{4},\ -\frac{5}{2},\ 7,\ 0.25\).
