🌱 Learn the concept

A rational number is any number that can be written as \(\frac{p}{q}\), where \(p,q\in\mathbb Z\) and \(q\ne0\).
🔢 Standard formWrite a rational number in lowest terms with a positive denominator.
📍 Number lineRational numbers can be represented at definite positions on the number line.
➕ OperationsAdd, subtract, multiply and divide rational numbers using sign and fraction rules.
⚖️ ComparisonUse a common denominator or cross multiplication to compare rational numbers.
