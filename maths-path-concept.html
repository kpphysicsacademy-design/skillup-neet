Operations on Rational Numbers

Learn the four arithmetic operations, see each rule worked step by step, explore examples interactively, then complete the dedicated mastery quiz when its question bank is available.

Step 1 • Learn the Concept

Rational numbers can be added, subtracted, multiplied, and divided using precise fraction rules.

Addition: use a common denominator. Subtraction: subtract numerators after using a common denominator.

\(\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad+bc}{bd}\)
\(\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad-bc}{bd}\)

Multiplication: multiply numerator by numerator and denominator by denominator.

\(\frac{a}{b}\times\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ac}{bd}\)

Division: multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor.

\(\frac{a}{b}\div\frac{c}{d}=\frac{a}{b}\times\frac{d}{c},\quad c\ne0\)

Important: simplify the final answer. Division by zero is undefined.
