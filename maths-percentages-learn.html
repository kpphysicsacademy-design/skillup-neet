Understanding Percentages

Percent means “per hundred”. Learn to convert, calculate, compare and apply percentages in everyday situations.

x% = x / 100

1. Percentage as a fraction

A percentage expresses a quantity out of 100. For example, 25% means 25 out of 100.

25% = 25/100 = 1/4

Useful equivalents: 10% = 1/10 · 20% = 1/5 · 25% = 1/4 · 50% = 1/2 · 75% = 3/4.

2. Convert fractions and decimals

Fraction → Percentage: × 100

Example: 3/4 × 100 = 75%.

Decimal → Percentage: × 100

Example: 0.36 × 100 = 36%. To convert a percentage to a decimal, divide by 100: 45% = 0.45.

3. Find a percentage of a number

x% of N = (x/100) × N

Example: 15% of 240 = 15/100 × 240 = 36.

Shortcut: 10% of 240 is 24 and 5% is 12. Add them to get 15% = 36.

4. Find what percentage one number is of another

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Example: 30 is what percent of 120? (30/120) × 100 = 25%.

5. Percentage increase and decrease

Change % = (Change / Original) × 100

Increase example: a value rises from 200 to 250. Increase = 50, so increase percentage = 50/200 × 100 = 25%.

Decrease example: a value falls from 200 to 150. Decrease = 50, so decrease percentage = 50/200 × 100 = 25%.

6. Discounts and sale price

Discount = Marked Price × Discount % / 100

For a ₹800 item with a 15% discount, discount = ₹120 and sale price = ₹680.

7. Find the original value

If a price after a 20% discount is ₹640, then ₹640 represents 80% of the original price.

Original price = 640 × 100 / 80 = ₹800

8. Successive percentage changes

For two successive changes, multiply the corresponding factors. A 10% increase followed by a 10% decrease gives:

1.10 × 0.90 = 0.99

The final value is 99% of the original: an overall decrease of 1%, not zero.
