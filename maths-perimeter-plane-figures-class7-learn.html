Perimeter of Plane Figures

Perimeter is the total length of the boundary of a closed plane figure. Imagine walking once around the outside edge.

Take QuizOpen Simulator

1. Square

A square has four equal sides. Add all four sides.

P = 4 × side

Example: side = 5 cm → P = 4 × 5 = 20 cm.

2. Rectangle

A rectangle has two equal lengths and two equal widths.

P = 2 × (length + width)

Example: length = 8 cm, width = 3 cm → P = 2 × (8 + 3) = 22 cm.

3. Triangle

Add the lengths of all three sides. For an equilateral triangle, all sides are equal.

P = a + b + c

Example: 4 cm + 5 cm + 6 cm = 15 cm. Equilateral triangle: P = 3 × side.

4. Regular polygons

A regular polygon has equal sides. Multiply one side length by the number of sides.

P = number of sides × side length
  • Pentagon: 5 × side
  • Hexagon: 6 × side
  • Octagon: 8 × side

5. Finding a missing side

Subtract the known side lengths from the total perimeter.

Missing side = P − sum of known sides

For a rectangle: width = P ÷ 2 − length.

6. Perimeter in daily life

Fencing a field, putting a border around a picture, or walking around a playground are perimeter situations. If you walk around twice, multiply the perimeter by 2.

Units matter: perimeter measures length, so use mm, cm, m or km. Do not use square units such as cm².

Quick method

  1. Identify the shape.
  2. Write down every boundary side.
  3. Use the suitable formula or add the sides.
  4. Check that the final unit is a length unit.
