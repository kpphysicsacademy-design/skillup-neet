Perimeter of Plane Figures

Perimeter is the total distance around the boundary of a closed plane figure. Use linear units such as mm, cm or m.

1. Square

All four sides are equal.

Perimeter = 4 × side

Example: side = 5 cm → perimeter = 4 × 5 = 20 cm.

2. Rectangle

Opposite sides are equal: two lengths and two widths.

Perimeter = 2 × (length + width)

Example: length = 8 cm, width = 3 cm → perimeter = 2 × 11 = 22 cm.

3. Triangle

Add the lengths of all three sides.

Perimeter = a + b + c

Example: sides 4 cm, 5 cm and 6 cm → perimeter = 15 cm. For an equilateral triangle, perimeter = 3 × side.

4. Regular polygons

A regular polygon has equal sides.

Perimeter = number of sides × side length
  • Regular pentagon: 5 × side
  • Regular hexagon: 6 × side
  • Regular octagon: 8 × side

5. Finding a missing side

When perimeter and other side lengths are known, subtract the known lengths from the total perimeter.

Missing side = Perimeter − sum of known sides

For a rectangle, if perimeter P and length l are known: width = P/2 − l.

6. Word problems

Fencing, border strips and walking around a field use perimeter. Count every boundary side once; multiply by the number of rounds if the path is repeated.

Remember: Perimeter measures length, so use units such as cm or m—not square units like cm².
