Polynomial Terminology

Brief Learn • Class 9 • Polynomials

📝 Practice Quiz🧪 Interactive Simulator

1. Variable and constant

A variable is a symbol whose value can change, such as x or y. A constant has a fixed value, such as 5 or −3. In a polynomial, a term without a variable is the constant term.

2. Terms and signs

Terms are the parts separated by plus or minus signs. Keep each sign with its term.

4x² − 3x + 7 → terms: 4x², −3x, 7

There are three terms. The constant term is 7.

3. Coefficient and variable part

The numerical coefficient is the signed number multiplying the variable part.

−5x³ → coefficient −5; variable part x³
x → coefficient 1
−y → coefficient −1
9 → constant term 9

The sign belongs to the coefficient.

4. Exponent and degree

The exponent tells how many times a variable is used as a factor. For a non-zero polynomial in one variable, degree is the greatest exponent with a non-zero coefficient after simplifying.

6x⁴ − 2x² + x − 8 → degree 4

5. Like and unlike terms

Like terms have exactly the same variable part with the same exponents. Their coefficients may differ. Unlike terms have different variable parts or powers.

Like: 3x², −5x², x²
Unlike: 2x, 2x², 2y

Only like terms can be combined by addition or subtraction: 3x² − 5x² = −2x².

6. Factors and product

Factors are quantities multiplied to form a term or expression.

6x²y = 6 × x × x × y

Here the factors are 6, x, x and y. In factorisation, a polynomial is written as a product of simpler factors.

7. Standard form and polynomial rules

  • Write terms in descending powers for standard form.
  • Polynomial powers are non-negative integers.
  • Negative or fractional powers of the variable are not allowed in a polynomial.
  • Combine like terms before identifying the final terms and degree.
  • The zero polynomial has all coefficients zero; its degree is not defined in the usual school convention.

Quick revision checklist

  • Identify the variable and constants.
  • Separate terms and retain their signs.
  • Read signed numerical coefficients correctly.
  • Find the constant term and exponent.
  • Group only terms with identical variable parts.
