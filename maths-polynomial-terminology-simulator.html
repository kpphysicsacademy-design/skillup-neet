MathJax formula guide

A polynomial in one variable can be written in standard form as:

\[P(x)=a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1x+a_0\]

The degree is the greatest exponent with a non-zero coefficient. For example:

\[P(x)=4x^3-2x^2+3x-5,\qquad \deg P=3\]

Like terms have identical variable parts and exponents. Their coefficients can differ.

\[3x^2-5x^2=(3-5)x^2=-2x^2\]
