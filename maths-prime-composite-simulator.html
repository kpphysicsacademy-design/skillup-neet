Prime and Composite Numbers

Explore factors, classify numbers, test whether a number is prime, and use a sieve to discover prime numbers.

1. Number classifier

Enter a positive whole number to see its factors and classification.

2. Prime tester

Check divisibility by possible factors up to the square root of the number.

3. Prime factorisation

Break a composite number into a product of prime factors.

4. Sieve of Eratosthenes

Generate all prime numbers up to your chosen limit. Green cells are prime; blue cells are composite.

5. Remember
