Operations on Rational Numbers

A rational number is p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

Addition and subtraction

Use a common denominator, then add or subtract numerators and simplify.

1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6
3/4 − 1/2 = 3/4 − 2/4 = 1/4
−2/5 + 1/5 = −1/5

Multiplication

Multiply numerators together and denominators together.

(2/3) × (3/5) = 6/15 = 2/5
(−2/3) × (−3/4) = 1/2

Division

Multiply by the reciprocal of the non-zero divisor. Division by zero is undefined.

(3/4) ÷ (2/5) = (3/4) × (5/2) = 15/8

Sign rules

(+)(+) = +　 (−)(−) = +
(+)(−) = −　 (−)(+) = −

These rules apply to multiplication and division.

Order of operations

Brackets first, then multiplication/division from left to right, then addition/subtraction from left to right.

(1/2 − 1/4) ÷ 1/2 = 1/4 ÷ 1/2 = 1/2

Properties

  • Addition and multiplication are commutative and associative.
  • Subtraction and division are not generally commutative or associative.
  • Zero is additive identity; one is multiplicative identity.
  • Every rational number has an additive inverse; every non-zero rational has a reciprocal.
  • Closure holds for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division by a non-zero rational.

Common mistakes

  • Do not add denominators.
  • Invert only the divisor when dividing.
  • Track signs carefully and follow operation order.
  • Simplify the final fraction.
