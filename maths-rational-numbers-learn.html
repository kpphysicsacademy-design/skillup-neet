Rational Numbers

A rational number is simply a number that can be written as a fraction.

p / q   where q ≠ 0

1. What do the numbers mean?

In a fraction, the top number tells how many parts we have. The bottom number tells how many equal parts make one whole.

3/4
4 = split one whole into 4 equal parts.
3 = take 3 of those parts.

2. Which numbers are rational?

✓
Fractions: 1/2, 3/5, −2/7
✓
Whole numbers: 4 = 4/1
✓
Decimals that stop or repeat: 0.5 = 1/2

Remember: the denominator (bottom number) must never be zero.

3. Positive and negative numbers

Positive numbers are to the right of zero. Negative numbers are to the left of zero.

−1   ←   0   →   +1
−1/2 is left of 0.   1/2 is right of 0.

4. Same value, different fraction

Multiply or divide the top and bottom by the same non-zero number. The value stays the same.

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6
All three fractions mean one-half.

5. Four operation reminders

+
Add: Make the bottom numbers the same, then add the top numbers.
1/4 + 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2
−
Subtract: Make the bottom numbers the same, then subtract the top numbers.
3/4 − 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2
×
Multiply: Top × top; bottom × bottom.
1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6
÷
Divide: Keep the first fraction, flip the second, then multiply.
1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 2

Remember these 3 things

  1. Write a rational number as p/q.
  2. Never put zero at the bottom.
  3. For equivalent fractions, change the top and bottom in the same way.
