Rationalisation

Make a surd denominator rational without changing the value of the fraction.

1. Single surd

Multiply numerator and denominator by the same surd.

1/√2 = √2/2

2. Conjugates

Change the sign between two terms to form the conjugate.

√5 + 2 ↔ √5 − 2

3. Difference of squares

Multiplying conjugates removes the surds from the product.

(a+b)(a−b) = a²−b²

4. Two surds

Use the conjugate of the full denominator.

1/(√5+√2) = (√5−√2)/3
Quick method
1. Identify the denominator. 2. Choose its surd or conjugate. 3. Multiply both numerator and denominator. 4. Simplify using (√a)² = a.
Remember
Multiply the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero expression. This keeps the fraction's value unchanged.
