🔬 Scientific Notation

Write very large and very small numbers in a short, clear form using powers of ten.

1. What is scientific notation?

Scientific notation (standard form) writes a number as a number from 1 up to, but not including, 10 multiplied by a power of 10.

a × 10ⁿ

1 ≤ a < 10, and n is an integer.

Example: 4,500,000 = 4.5 × 10⁶

2. Convert a large number

Step 1Place the decimal after the first non-zero digit.
Step 2Count how many places the decimal moved left.
Step 3Use that count as a positive exponent.
Step 4Write the number as coefficient × 10ⁿ.
72,000,000
Move decimal 7 places left → 7.2
Answer: 7.2 × 10⁷

3. Convert a small decimal

For a number smaller than 1, move the decimal right until the coefficient is between 1 and 10. The exponent is negative.

0.00072
Move decimal 4 places right → 7.2
Answer: 7.2 × 10⁻⁴
💡 Large number → positive exponent. Small decimal (between 0 and 1) → negative exponent.

4. Change back to ordinary form

Positive exponent

Move the decimal to the right.

3.4 × 10³ = 3,400
Negative exponent

Move the decimal to the left.

5.6 × 10⁻³ = 0.0056

5. Calculate with scientific notation

Multiplication:
(2 × 10³)(3 × 10²)
Multiply coefficients and add powers:
6 × 10⁵
Division:
(8 × 10⁶) ÷ (2 × 10²)
Divide coefficients and subtract powers:
4 × 10⁴

6. Quick check

Write 0.00056 in scientific notation.
5.6 × 10⁻⁴ (decimal moves 4 places right).
