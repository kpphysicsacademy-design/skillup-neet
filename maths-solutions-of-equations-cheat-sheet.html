Solutions of Equations

1. Check a solution

Substitute both coordinates. For x+y=5, (2,3) works because 2+3=5.

2. Find a missing coordinate

For x+2y=10 and x=4: 2y=6, so y=3.

3. Intercepts

For 2x−3y=12, set x=0 to obtain y=−4; set y=0 to obtain x=6.

4. Number of solutions

A linear equation in two variables with at least one nonzero variable coefficient has infinitely many real solutions, forming a straight line.

5. Common solutions

For x+y=7 and x−y=1, add to obtain 2x=8. Then x=4 and y=3. Check both equations.

6. Sign check

For 3x−y=7, (2,−1) works: 6−(−1)=7.
