Standard Algebraic Identities

Understand the key identities, expand expressions and use patterns to calculate quickly.

1. Square of a sum

\((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\)

Square the first term, add twice the product of both terms, then add the square of the second term.

Example:
\((x+3)^2=x^2+2(x)(3)+3^2\)
\(=x^2+6x+9\)

2. Square of a difference

\((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\)

The middle term is negative. The last term is still positive because the second term is squared.

Example:
\((m-5)^2=m^2-2(m)(5)+25\)
\(=m^2-10m+25\)

3. Difference of two squares

\((a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2\)

The two middle products cancel each other.

Example:
\((x+4)(x-4)=x^2-16\)

4. Product of two binomials

\((x+a)(x+b)=x^2+(a+b)x+ab\)

Add the constants to get the coefficient of x. Multiply the constants to get the final term.

Example:
\((x+4)(x+6)=x^2+10x+24\)

5. Square of three terms

\((a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2ab+2bc+2ca\)

Square each term and add twice each distinct pairwise product.

6. Useful derived identities

\(a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-2ab\)
\(a^2+b^2=(a-b)^2+2ab\)
\((a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab\)

7. Mental calculation example

Find \(103\times97\).

\(=(100+3)(100-3)\)
\(=100^2-3^2\)
\(=10000-9=9991\)

8. Check your understanding

Expand \((2x+3)^2\).

\((2x)^2+2(2x)(3)+3^2=4x^2+12x+9\)
