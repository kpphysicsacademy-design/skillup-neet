Standard Identities

Your colourful one-page formula cheat sheet. Learn the pattern, remember the sign, and apply it.

⭐ Golden rule: square the first term, multiply both terms, then square the last term.
1

Square of a Sum

\((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\)

Middle term is positive.

Example:
\((x+3)^2\)
\(=x^2+6x+9\)
2

Square of a Difference

\((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\)

Middle term is negative; last term stays positive.

Example:
\((m-5)^2\)
\(=m^2-10m+25\)
3

Difference of Squares

\((a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2\)

Same terms, opposite signs. Middle terms cancel.

Example:
\((x+4)(x-4)=x^2-16\)
4

Product of Binomials

\((x+a)(x+b)=x^2+(a+b)x+ab\)

Add constants for the x coefficient; multiply them for the last term.

Example:
\((x+4)(x+6)\)
\(=x^2+10x+24\)
5

Square of Three Terms

\((a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2ab+2bc+2ca\)

Square each term, then add twice every different pairwise product.

6

Useful Derived Identities

\(\ a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-2ab\ \)
\(\ a^2+b^2=(a-b)^2+2ab\ \)
\(\ (a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab\ \)
7

Fast Mental Calculation

Use difference of squares to calculate numbers close to a round number.

\(\begin{aligned}103\times97&=(100+3)(100-3)\\&=100^2-3^2\\&=10000-9\\&=\boxed{9991}\end{aligned}\)

🧠 Remember the signs

Plus square
\(+2ab\) in the middle
Minus square
\(-2ab\) in the middle
Last term
\(+b^2\) in both squares
Difference
\(a^2-b^2\), not \(a^2+b^2\)
