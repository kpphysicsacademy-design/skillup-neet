The same values are used in all four identity examples below.

1. Square of a sum

\((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\)
\((5+2)^2=49\)
\(25+20+4=49\)

2. Square of a difference

\((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\)
\((5-2)^2=9\)
\(25-20+4=9\)

3. Difference of squares

\((a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2\)
\((5+2)(5-2)=21\)
\(25-4=21\)

4. Product of two binomials

\((x+a)(x+b)=x^2+(a+b)x+ab\)
\((3+5)(3+2)=40\)
