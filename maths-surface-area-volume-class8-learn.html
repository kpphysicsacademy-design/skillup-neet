Surface Area & Volume

Learn cubes and cuboids with simple pictures, easy formulas and solved examples.

1. Meet the shapes

2. Surface area means covering

Surface area is the total area of the outside faces. Think of wrapping a gift box or painting its outside.

Cube TSA = 6a²
Cuboid TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl)
Cube LSA = 4a²

TSA = total surface area (all faces). LSA = lateral surface area (side faces only).

3. Volume means space inside

Volume tells how much space a solid occupies or how much it can hold.

Cube volume = a³
Cuboid volume = l × b × h

Surface area uses square units (cm², m²). Volume uses cubic units (cm³, m³).

4. Easy solved examples

Example 1 — Cube
Side = 4 cm
Volume = 4 × 4 × 4 = 64 cm³
Total surface area = 6 × 4² = 96 cm²
Example 2 — Cuboid
l = 5 cm, b = 3 cm, h = 2 cm
Volume = 5 × 3 × 2 = 30 cm³
TSA = 2(15 + 6 + 10) = 62 cm²

5. Capacity conversion

1 cm³ = 1 mL
1,000 cm³ = 1 L
1 m³ = 1,000 L

A tank of 3 m³ can hold 3,000 litres.

Quick check

A cube has side 3 cm. What is its volume?

3³ = 3 × 3 × 3 = 27 cm³.
