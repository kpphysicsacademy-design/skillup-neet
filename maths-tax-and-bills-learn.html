Tax and Bills

Learn how to calculate tax, GST amounts, subtotals and final bills. Practice examples use stated hypothetical tax rates; actual GST rates depend on the goods or services and applicable rules.

Tax = Taxable value × Tax rate / 100

1. Understand a bill

  • Item price: price of each product or service.
  • Quantity: number of units purchased.
  • Subtotal: sum of item prices after multiplying by quantities.
  • Taxable value: value on which tax is calculated.
  • Tax amount: taxable value multiplied by the stated rate.
  • Total bill: amount payable after adding applicable charges and tax.

2. Calculate a subtotal

Subtotal = (Price₁ × Quantity₁) + (Price₂ × Quantity₂) + …

Example: 3 notebooks at ₹40 and 2 pens at ₹10. Subtotal = (3 × 40) + (2 × 10) = ₹140.

3. Calculate tax

Tax = Taxable value × Rate / 100

Example using a hypothetical 5% rate: tax on ₹800 = 800 × 5/100 = ₹40.

Total = Taxable value + Tax

Total = ₹800 + ₹40 = ₹840.

4. Find the tax rate

Tax rate = (Tax amount / Taxable value) × 100

Example: tax is ₹60 on a taxable value of ₹600. Rate = 60/600 × 100 = 10%.

5. Find price before tax

When the total already includes tax, divide by the multiplier.

Pre-tax value = Tax-inclusive total / (1 + rate/100)

Example: total ₹1,180 at a hypothetical 18% rate. Pre-tax value = 1,180/1.18 = ₹1,000.

6. GST split in an intra-state example

For an illustrative intra-state supply with a total GST rate of 18%, the tax is commonly represented as equal CGST and SGST components when the applicable split is equal.

CGST 9% + SGST 9% = GST 18%

For an inter-state supply, IGST is generally shown instead. Use the rate and tax type stated in the question or invoice.

7. Read an invoice

A tax invoice can show supplier details, invoice number and date, description and quantity, total value, taxable value, tax rate, tax amount and place of supply where applicable.

In real transactions, check the invoice and the applicable GST classification rather than assuming every product has the same rate.

8. Final bill checklist

  1. Multiply each unit price by quantity.
  2. Add item amounts to get the subtotal.
  3. Identify the taxable value and stated tax rate.
  4. Calculate the tax amount.
  5. Add tax and any other stated charges to find the total payable.
  6. Check the arithmetic and currency units.
