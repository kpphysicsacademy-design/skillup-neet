Types of Quadrilaterals

A quadrilateral is a closed polygon with four sides, four vertices and four interior angles. Its properties depend on side lengths, parallel sides, angles and diagonals.

Interior-angle sum = 360°

1. Parallelogram

A quadrilateral with both pairs of opposite sides parallel.

Opposite sides are parallel and equal.
Opposite angles are equal.
Adjacent angles add to 180°.
Diagonals bisect each other.
Example: If one angle is 70°, its adjacent angle is 110° and its opposite angle is 70°.

2. Rectangle

A parallelogram in which all four angles are right angles.

Opposite sides are equal and parallel.
Every angle is 90°.
Diagonals are equal.
Diagonals bisect each other.

3. Rhombus

A parallelogram with all four sides equal.

All sides are equal.
Opposite angles are equal.
Diagonals bisect each other at 90°.
Diagonals bisect the opposite angles.

A rhombus does not need to have four right angles. A square is a special rhombus.

4. Square

A square is both a rectangle and a rhombus: all four sides are equal and all four angles are 90°.

All sides equal.
All angles 90°.
Diagonals equal.
Diagonals perpendicular and bisect each other and the angles.

5. Trapezium

In the common school definition used in this lesson, a trapezium has exactly one pair of opposite sides parallel.

Isosceles trapezium: The non-parallel sides (legs) are equal. Its base angles are equal and its diagonals are equal.

6. Kite

A kite has two pairs of equal adjacent sides.

One diagonal bisects the other.
Diagonals are perpendicular.
One pair of opposite angles is equal.
The symmetry diagonal bisects a pair of opposite angles.

7. Compare the quadrilaterals

Parallelogram
Two pairs of opposite sides parallel; opposite sides equal.
Rectangle
Parallelogram with four right angles; diagonals equal.
Rhombus
Parallelogram with four equal sides; diagonals perpendicular.
Square
Both rectangle and rhombus properties.
Trapezium
Exactly one pair of opposite sides parallel (school convention).
Kite
Two pairs of equal adjacent sides.

8. Useful tests for a parallelogram

  • Both pairs of opposite sides are parallel.
  • Both pairs of opposite sides are equal.
  • One pair of opposite sides is both equal and parallel.
  • Diagonals bisect each other.
  • Both pairs of opposite angles are equal.

9. Common mistakes

  • Every square is a rectangle and a rhombus, but not every rectangle or rhombus is a square.
  • A rhombus does not always have right angles.
  • A rectangle's diagonals are equal, but need not be perpendicular.
  • A kite's diagonals are perpendicular, but are not necessarily equal or mutual bisectors.
  • Do not confuse equal adjacent sides with equal opposite sides.
