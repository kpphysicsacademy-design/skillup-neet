Types of Triangles

Brief Learn · Classify by sides and angles

1. Classification by sides

Triangles are classified by comparing the lengths of their three sides.

Equilateral
3 equal sides
Isosceles
At least 2 equal sides
Scalene
3 unequal sides

An equilateral triangle also has three equal angles of 60°. An isosceles triangle has equal angles opposite its equal sides.

2. Classification by angles

Acute-angled
All angles below 90°
Right-angled
One angle is 90°
Obtuse-angled
One angle above 90°

A triangle can have only one right angle or one obtuse angle. The other two angles must be acute.

3. A triangle can have two classifications

One classification describes its sides and another describes its angles.

5 cm, 12 cm, 13 cm
Scalene and right-angled

All sides are unequal. Also, 5² + 12² = 13², so it is right-angled.

4. Angle and side reminders

  • Interior angles of every triangle total 180°.
  • Equal sides have equal opposite angles.
  • The sum of any two sides must be greater than the third side.
  • An equilateral triangle is also acute-angled.
Common mistake: “isosceles” describes side lengths, while “acute”, “right” and “obtuse” describe angles. A triangle may have one label from each group.
