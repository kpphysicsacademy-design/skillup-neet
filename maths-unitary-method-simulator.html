Unitary Method Simulator

Find the value of one unit first, then scale up or down. Explore direct proportion and inverse proportion through everyday examples.

1. One-unit value explorer

Enter the total quantity and its total cost or amount. The simulator finds the value of one unit and then the value of your chosen quantity.

2. Direct proportion explorer

When one quantity increases or decreases by a factor, the other changes by the same factor (at a constant rate).

More units → more valueFewer units → less value

3. Inverse proportion explorer

For the same job, more workers or machines mean fewer days or hours. The product stays constant when all work at the same rate.

4. Compare direct and inverse proportion

Select a situation to see which relationship applies and why.

5. Everyday unitary challenge

If 5 notebooks cost ₹150, what is the cost of 8 notebooks?

Use two steps: find one unit, then multiply.
