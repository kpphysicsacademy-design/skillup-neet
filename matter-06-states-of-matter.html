Matter and Its Nature • Learn

States of matter

Solid: fixed shape and fixed volume; particles are closely packed.

Liquid: fixed volume but no fixed shape; it takes the shape of its container.

Gas: neither fixed shape nor fixed volume; particles are widely separated and move freely.
