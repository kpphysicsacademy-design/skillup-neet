1. What does absolute uncertainty describe?

Key point: Absolute uncertainty gives an estimated range or amount of doubt in a measured value.

Answer: The estimated amount by which a measurement may vary

2. Absolute uncertainty in a length is usually written in:

Key point: Absolute uncertainty carries the same unit as the measured quantity.

Answer: The same unit as the length

3. A length is reported as 12.0 ± 0.2 cm. What is the absolute uncertainty?

Key point: The number after ± is the absolute uncertainty.

Answer: 0.2 cm

4. A reading is 5.4 s with uncertainty 0.1 s. The result is written as:

Key point: The value and its absolute uncertainty should use consistent units.

Answer: (5.4 ± 0.1) s

5. Repeated readings are 10.2, 10.4, and 10.3 cm. Their mean is:

Key point: Mean = (10.2 + 10.4 + 10.3)/3 = 10.3 cm.

Answer: 10.3 cm
