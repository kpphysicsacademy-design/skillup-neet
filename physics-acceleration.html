Acceleration

A short lesson: meaning, formula and one example.

📘 Learn🎮 Simulator🧠 Practice

1. Meaning

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time.

a = (v − u) / t

SI unit: m/s² • Dimension: [LT⁻²] • Vector quantity.

2. Important formula

v = u + at

u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time.

3. Simple example

Velocity changes from 4 m/s to 16 m/s in 3 s.
a = (16 − 4) ÷ 3
a = 4 m/s².

Remember

• Positive or negative signs show direction.
• Zero acceleration means constant velocity.
• Acceleration opposite to velocity reduces speed.
• Slope of a velocity–time graph gives acceleration.
