Study notes are built from the explanations and correct answers in this topic's existing question bank.

1. What does angular acceleration describe?

Key point: Angular acceleration measures how quickly angular velocity changes.

Answer: Rate of change of angular velocity

2. What is the SI unit of angular acceleration?

Key point: Angular acceleration is measured in radians per second squared.

Answer: rad s⁻²

3. The average angular acceleration is calculated using:

Key point: Average α equals change in angular velocity divided by elapsed time.

Answer: α = (ω − ω₀)/t

4. A wheel's angular velocity changes from 2 to 10 rad s⁻¹ in 4 s. Find α.

Key point: α = (10 − 2)/4 = 2 rad s⁻².

Answer: 2 rad s⁻²

5. A rotor speeds up from rest at 3 rad s⁻² for 5 s. Final angular velocity?

Key point: ω = ω₀ + αt = 0 + 3×5 = 15 rad s⁻¹.

Answer: 15 rad s⁻¹
