1. Angular displacement is the change in:

Key point: Angular displacement is final angular position minus initial angular position.

Answer: Angular position

2. The SI unit of angular displacement is:

Key point: Angular displacement is measured in radians (rad).

Answer: Radian

3. One complete revolution equals:

Key point: A full revolution is 360° = 2π rad.

Answer: 2π rad

4. A quarter revolution equals:

Key point: One quarter of 2π is π/2 rad.

Answer: π/2 rad

5. Convert 180° to radians.

Key point: 180° × π/180° = π rad.

Answer: π rad
