SkillUp Physics Easy Mode: Angular Momentum quiz Explore Angular Momentum in Physics, understand the key relationships and practise focused questions with Ski

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. Angular momentum of a particle about a point is defined by:

Key point: Angular momentum is the vector product of position and linear momentum.

Answer: L = r × p

2. For a rigid body rotating about a fixed principal axis, angular momentum is:

Key point: For fixed-axis rotation about a principal axis, L = Iω.

Answer: L = Iω

3. The SI unit of angular momentum is:

Key point: Angular momentum has units of moment of inertia times angular velocity.

Answer: kg m² s⁻¹

4. The dimensions of angular momentum are:

Key point: L = Iω has dimensions ML² × T⁻¹.

Answer: ML²T⁻¹

5. The direction of angular momentum for fixed-axis rotation is found using the:

Key point: Curl your right-hand fingers in the rotation direction; the thumb gives the axis direction.

Answer: Right-hand rule
