Practice angular momentum of a rigid body with 30 interactive Physics questions.

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. For a rigid body rotating about a fixed symmetry axis, angular momentum magnitude is:

Key point: For fixed-axis rotation about a symmetry axis, L = Iω, with I measured about that same axis.

Answer: L = Iω

2. What is the SI unit of angular momentum?

Key point: Angular momentum has dimensions ML²/T and SI unit kg m²/s (equivalently J s).

Answer: kg m²/s

3. Angular momentum is a:

Key point: Angular momentum has magnitude and direction; its direction follows the cross product or right-hand rule.

Answer: Vector quantity

4. For a particle about a chosen origin, angular momentum is:

Key point: The vector definition is L = r × p.

Answer: r × p

5. The magnitude of particle angular momentum is:

Key point: The magnitude of r × p is rp sinθ, where θ is the angle between r and p.

Answer: rp sinθ
