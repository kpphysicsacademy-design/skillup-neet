SkillUp Physics Easy Mode: Angular Velocity quiz Explore Angular Velocity in Physics, understand the key relationships and practise focused questions with Ski

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. Angular velocity is the rate of change of:

Key point: Angular velocity tells how quickly angular position changes: ω = Δθ/Δt for average angular velocity.

Answer: Angular position with time

2. The SI unit of angular velocity is:

Key point: Angular velocity is measured in radians per second (rad/s).

Answer: rad/s

3. Average angular velocity is calculated using:

Key point: Average angular velocity equals angular displacement divided by elapsed time.

Answer: ω = Δθ/Δt

4. A wheel turns through 6 rad in 2 s. Its average angular velocity is:

Key point: ω = Δθ/Δt = 6/2 = 3 rad/s.

Answer: 3 rad/s

5. A disc rotates through 10 rad in 5 s. Its average angular velocity is:

Key point: ω = 10/5 = 2 rad/s.

Answer: 2 rad/s
