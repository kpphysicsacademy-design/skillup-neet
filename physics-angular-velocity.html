Angular Velocity

Understand how fast angular position changes and how rotation connects to linear motion.

ROTATIONAL MOTIONSHORT LESSON

1. Meaning of angular velocity

Angular velocity describes the rate of change of angular position with time. Its direction is along the axis of rotation, determined by the right-hand rule.

ωavg = Δθ / Δt

Instantaneous angular velocity is ω = dθ/dt. Its SI unit is radian per second (rad/s).

2. Sign and direction

With anticlockwise taken as positive, anticlockwise angular velocity is positive and clockwise angular velocity is negative. Angular velocity is a vector quantity.

ω = +3 rad/s or −3 rad/s

3. Frequency and time period

Frequency is the number of revolutions per second. Time period is the time for one revolution.

ω = 2πf = 2π/T
f = 2 Hz → ω = 4π rad/s
T = 4 s → ω = π/2 rad/s
60 rpm = 1 revolution/s → ω = 2π rad/s

4. Angular and tangential speed

For a point at distance r from a fixed axis, tangential speed is related to angular speed by:

v = rω

All points on a rigid body rotating about a fixed axis have the same angular velocity, but points farther from the axis have greater tangential speed.

5. Worked example

A wheel rotates through 6 rad in 2 s. Find its average angular velocity.

ω = 6 / 2 = 3 rad/s

If the rotation is anticlockwise, the signed angular velocity is +3 rad/s.

Quick check

A disc rotates at 2 revolutions per second. What is its angular speed?

ω = 2πf = 2π × 2 = 4π rad/s.
