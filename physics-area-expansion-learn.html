📐 Area Expansion · Learn

Understand how a solid's surface area changes when its temperature changes.

1. Meaning of area expansion

Area expansion is the increase or decrease in the surface area of a solid due to a change in temperature. When an isotropic solid is heated uniformly, its length and surface dimensions generally increase.

2. Area expansion formula

ΔA = γA₀ΔT
  • ΔA — change in area
  • γ — coefficient of area expansion
  • A₀ — initial area
  • ΔT — change in temperature

3. Relation with linear expansion

γ ≈ 2α

For an isotropic solid and small temperature changes, the coefficient of area expansion is approximately twice the coefficient of linear expansion.

4. Quick example

A sheet has an initial area of 2 m², γ = 2 × 10⁻⁵ K⁻¹ and its temperature rises by 10 K.

ΔA = 2 × 10⁻⁵ × 2 × 10 = 0.0004 m²

Remember

A hole in a uniformly heated plate expands as though the hole were filled with the same material. Area expansion depends on initial area, the coefficient and the temperature change.
