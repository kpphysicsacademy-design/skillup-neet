🏃 Average Speed · Learn

Understand how total path length and elapsed time determine average speed.

📌 Quick Cheat Sheet

Average speed is total distance travelled divided by total elapsed time. It is a nonnegative scalar with SI unit m/s.

Average speed = total distance ÷ total time

SituationRule
Several journey legsΣ distances / Σ times
Equal distances at speeds u, v2uv/(u+v), for positive u,v
Equal time intervalsArithmetic mean of the speeds
m/s → km/hMultiply by 3.6
Round tripAverage speed may be positive even if average velocity is zero

Worked example: 60 m in 10 s and 40 m in 20 s → total distance 100 m; total time 30 s; average speed ≈ 3.33 m/s.

Equal-distance example: 60 km at 30 km/h and 60 km at 60 km/h → total 120 km in 3 h → 40 km/h, not 45 km/h.

NEET tip: do not average speeds directly unless the time intervals are equal. Time must be positive.
