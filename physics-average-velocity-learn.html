Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. Average velocity is defined as…

Key point: Average velocity equals total displacement divided by total elapsed time.

Answer: Displacement ÷ elapsed time

2. A runner moves 100 m east in 20 s. Average velocity is…

Key point: Average velocity = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s east.

Answer: 5 m/s east

3. A person returns to the starting point after a walk. Average velocity for the whole trip is…

Key point: Net displacement is zero, so average velocity is zero.

Answer: Zero

4. The SI unit of average velocity is…

Key point: Velocity is displacement per unit time, measured in metres per second.

Answer: m/s

5. A car has displacement 60 m north in 12 s. Its average velocity is…

Key point: 60 m ÷ 12 s = 5 m/s north.

Answer: 5 m/s north
