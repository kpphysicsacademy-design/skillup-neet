Average Velocity

A quick lesson: learn the formula, meaning and one example.

📘 Learn🎮 Simulator🧠 Practice

1. Meaning

Average velocity is displacement divided by total time. It tells how fast position changes and in which direction.

Average velocity = Displacement ÷ Time

SI unit: m/s • Dimension: [LT⁻¹] • It is a vector.

2. Formula

v̄ = (x₂ − x₁) ÷ Δt

Final position minus initial position, divided by elapsed time.

3. Simple example

A student moves from 5 m to 25 m in 4 s.
Displacement = 25 − 5 = 20 m
Average velocity = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 m/s.

Remember

  • Use displacement, not distance.
  • Negative velocity means opposite direction.
  • On a round trip, average velocity is zero.
