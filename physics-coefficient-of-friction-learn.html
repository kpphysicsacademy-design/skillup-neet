Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. What does the coefficient of friction describe?

Key point: In the simple dry-friction model, the coefficient relates friction magnitude to the normal reaction.

Answer: How much frictional force is available relative to the normal force

2. The coefficient of static friction is commonly written as:

Key point: The symbol μₛ denotes the coefficient of static friction.

Answer: μₛ

3. The coefficient of kinetic friction is commonly written as:

Key point: The symbol μₖ denotes the coefficient used when surfaces slide relative to each other.

Answer: μₖ

4. The coefficient of friction has:

Key point: It is a ratio of forces, so the units cancel.

Answer: No units

5. In the basic model, kinetic friction is calculated using:

Key point: Kinetic friction magnitude is the kinetic coefficient multiplied by the normal reaction.

Answer: fₖ = μₖN
