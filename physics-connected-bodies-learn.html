Practice connected bodies with 30 step-by-step physics questions. Explore Connected Bodies in Physics, understand the key relationships and practise focused q

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. What is a connected-body system?

Key point: A string, pulley or other connection constrains how the connected objects move.

Answer: Two or more objects linked so their motions are related

2. For an ideal light, inextensible string over a smooth fixed pulley, the tension is:

Key point: In the ideal model, the string and pulley make the tension uniform throughout the string.

Answer: The same on both sides

3. Two blocks connected by a taut inextensible string over an ideal pulley have acceleration magnitudes that are:

Key point: The fixed string length constrains both blocks to have equal acceleration magnitudes.

Answer: Equal

4. A 2 kg block on a smooth table is connected over an ideal pulley to a hanging 3 kg block. Take g = 10 m/s². The acceleration magnitude is:

Key point: The net driving force is 3g and total mass is 2 + 3 kg, so a = 30/5 = 6 m/s².

Answer: 6 m/s²

5. For the system in the previous question, the string tension is:

Key point: For the 2 kg block on the smooth table, T = ma = 2 × 6 = 12 N.

Answer: 12 N
