📘 Conservation of Angular Momentum | Practice | SkillUp Physics · Learn

Practice conservation of angular momentum with 30 questions and explanations.

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. Angular momentum is conserved when the net external torque is:

Key point: With zero net external torque, total angular momentum remains constant.

Answer: Zero

2. For a rotating rigid body about a fixed axis, angular momentum is:

Key point: For fixed-axis rotation, L = Iω.

Answer: L = Iω

3. A skater pulls in their arms, reducing moment of inertia. With negligible external torque, angular speed:

Key point: Iω remains constant, so a smaller I means a larger ω.

Answer: Increases

4. A system has I = 4 kg m² and ω = 3 rad s⁻¹. Its angular momentum is:

Key point: L = Iω = 4×3 = 12 kg m² s⁻¹.

Answer: 12 kg m² s⁻¹

5. A rotating body has initial I = 6 kg m² and ω = 2 rad s⁻¹. If I becomes 3 kg m², final ω is:

Key point: I₁ω₁ = I₂ω₂, so ω₂ = (6×2)/3 = 4 rad s⁻¹.

Answer: 4 rad s⁻¹
