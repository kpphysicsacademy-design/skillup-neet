Conservation of Angular Momentum

Change the moment of inertia and watch the angular speed adjust while angular momentum stays constant.

Rotating system

This fixed-axis model assumes negligible external torque.

Final angular speed: 7.50 rad s⁻¹

Final angular speed ω₂7.50rad s⁻¹
Initial angular momentum L₁15.00kg m² s⁻¹
Final angular momentum L₂15.00kg m² s⁻¹
Initial rotational KE22.50J
Final rotational KE56.25J
I₁ω₁ = I₂ω₂   ⇒   ω₂ = I₁ω₁ / I₂
I decreases, so angular speed increases. Angular momentum remains constant.

What to observe

Angular momentum stays constant in this model, but rotational kinetic energy can change when the system's moment of inertia changes. Internal work can transfer energy within the system.
