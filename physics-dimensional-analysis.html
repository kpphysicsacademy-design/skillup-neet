Dimensional Analysis

Use dimensions to check physical equations and derive relations.

What are dimensions?

Dimensions show how a physical quantity depends on fundamental quantities such as mass, length and time.

[Q] = [Mᵃ Lᵇ Tᶜ]
MMass
LLength
TTime

Common dimensions

Velocity[LT⁻¹]
Acceleration[LT⁻²]
Force[MLT⁻²]
Work / Energy[ML²T⁻²]
Power[ML²T⁻³]
Pressure[ML⁻¹T⁻²]

Uses

  • Check whether an equation is dimensionally correct.
  • Derive relations between physical quantities.
  • Convert units from one system to another.

Principle of homogeneity

Every term in a valid physical equation must have the same dimensions. The dimensions on the left and right sides must match.

Example: v = u + at
[v] = [LT⁻¹]
[u] = [LT⁻¹]
[at] = [LT⁻²] × [T] = [LT⁻¹]
All terms match — dimensionally correct.
