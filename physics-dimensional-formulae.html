Dimensional Formulae

Express physical quantities using fundamental dimensions.

📌 Quick Cheat Sheet

Express physical quantities as powers of fundamental dimensions such as mass M, length L and time T.

QuantityDimensional formula
Velocity[LT⁻¹]
Acceleration[LT⁻²]
Force[MLT⁻²]
Momentum[MLT⁻¹]
Work[ML²T⁻²]
Power[ML²T⁻³]
Pressure[ML⁻¹T⁻²]
Density[ML⁻³]

Worked example: Pressure = force ÷ area → [MLT⁻²]/[L²] = [ML⁻¹T⁻²].

Exam tip: dimensionless quantities have formula [1]; a dimensional formula is not a unit.

How to derive

Replace each quantity in its defining equation with its dimensions, then simplify the powers.

Force = mass × acceleration
[F] = M × LT⁻² = [MLT⁻²]
Pressure = force ÷ area
[P] = MLT⁻² ÷ L² = [ML⁻¹T⁻²]

Remember

  • Dimensional formulae are not SI units.
  • Work and torque share the same dimensions.
  • Dimensional analysis cannot find pure numerical constants.
  • Dimensional correctness does not guarantee physical correctness.
