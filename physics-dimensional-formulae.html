Dimensional Formulae

Express physical quantities using fundamental dimensions.

Fundamental dimensions

MMass
LLength
TTime
IElectric current
ΘTemperature
[Q] = Mᵃ Lᵇ Tᶜ

The powers a, b and c are dimensional exponents.

Common dimensional formulae

Velocity[LT⁻¹]
Acceleration[LT⁻²]
Force[MLT⁻²]
Momentum[MLT⁻¹]
Work / Energy[ML²T⁻²]
Power[ML²T⁻³]
Pressure[ML⁻¹T⁻²]
Density[ML⁻³]
Frequency[T⁻¹]
Gravitational constant[M⁻¹L³T⁻²]
Surface tension[MT⁻²]
Dynamic viscosity[ML⁻¹T⁻¹]

How to derive

Replace each quantity in its defining equation with its dimensions, then simplify the powers.

Force = mass × acceleration
[F] = M × LT⁻² = [MLT⁻²]
Pressure = force ÷ area
[P] = MLT⁻² ÷ L² = [ML⁻¹T⁻²]

Remember

  • Dimensional formulae are not SI units.
  • Work and torque share the same dimensions.
  • Dimensional analysis cannot find pure numerical constants.
  • Dimensional correctness does not guarantee physical correctness.
