📘 Learn: Distance

Distance tells us how much ground an object covers during its motion. It depends on the actual route, not just the starting and ending points.

1. Meaning of distance

The total length of the actual path followed by a moving object is called distance.

2. Scalar quantity

Distance has magnitude only. It does not have a direction, so it is a scalar quantity.

3. SI unit

The SI unit is metre (m). Other common units include centimetre and kilometre.

4. Distance is path-dependent

A curved or zigzag route can be longer than a straight route between the same endpoints.

5. Distance and displacement

Distance is the complete path length. Distance is always greater than or equal to the magnitude of displacement.

6. Key rule

For separate parts of a journey, add the lengths of every part. Never cancel backward motion when calculating distance.
