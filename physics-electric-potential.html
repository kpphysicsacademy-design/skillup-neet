⚡ Electric Potential

Learn Gauss’s law step by step: electric flux, enclosed charge, Gaussian surfaces, and applications to spherical, cylindrical and planar symmetry. Remember: net flux through a closed surface is determined by the net charge enclosed.

\\[\\oint_S \\vec E\\cdot d\\vec A=\\frac{Q_{\\text{enclosed}}}{\\varepsilon_0}\\]

Net electric flux through a closed surface = net enclosed charge ÷ permittivity of free space.
