Equation of State

Build the equation of state from the molecular picture of matter. In an ideal gas, microscopic molecular motion produces pressure, while temperature represents the average translational kinetic energy.

PV = nRT
🔬 Core molecular connection: Molecules move randomly and continuously. When they collide with the container walls, they transfer momentum and produce pressure. Higher temperature means greater average translational kinetic energy. The macroscopic variables P, V, T and n describe the collective behaviour of microscopic particles.
💥 Pressure P comes from molecular collisions with the walls.
🌡️ Temperature T sets average translational kinetic energy.
⚛️ Amount n represents the quantity of gas particles.
🧠 Molecular-theory bridge
P = ⅓ρc² → PV = ⅓Nmc²
Since ½mc² = ³⁄₂kT, we obtain PV = NkT = nRT.
This connects the Molecular Nature of Matter chapter directly to the macroscopic equation of state.
