Equation of State

Connect the macroscopic state of a gas with its molecular picture. Pressure arises from molecular collisions with the container, while temperature measures the average translational kinetic energy of gas molecules.

PV = nRT
🔬 Molecular connection: For an ideal gas, molecules are treated as tiny particles in continuous random motion. Their collisions with the walls produce pressure. Increasing temperature increases their average kinetic energy; the equation of state connects this microscopic picture with measurable P, V, T and n.
