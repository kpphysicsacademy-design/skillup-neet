Practice questions on equations of motion. Explore Equations of Motion in Physics, understand the key relationships and practise focused questions with SkillU

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. Which equation relates final velocity, initial velocity, acceleration and time?

Key point: For constant acceleration, final velocity is initial velocity plus acceleration multiplied by time.

Answer: v = u + at

2. For constant acceleration, displacement can be calculated using…

Key point: The displacement equation is s = ut + ½at².

Answer: s = ut + ½at²

3. Which equation does not contain time?

Key point: v² = u² + 2as relates velocity, acceleration and displacement without time.

Answer: v² = u² + 2as

4. A body starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Final velocity is…

Key point: v = u + at = 0 + 2 × 5 = 10 m/s.

Answer: 10 m/s

5. A car has u = 4 m/s, a = 3 m/s² and t = 2 s. Final velocity is…

Key point: v = 4 + 3 × 2 = 10 m/s.

Answer: 10 m/s
