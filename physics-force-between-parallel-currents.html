⚡ Force Between Parallel Currents

Master attraction and repulsion, Ampere’s force law, current dependence and numerical applications.

1. What happens when two parallel wires carry current?

A current-carrying wire produces a magnetic field around it. When a second current-carrying wire is placed nearby, that magnetic field exerts a force on the second wire.

  • Same direction currents: the wires attract.
  • Opposite direction currents: the wires repel.
Memory trick: Same direction → attraction. Opposite direction → repulsion.

2. Deriving the force law

Consider two long straight parallel wires separated by distance d, carrying currents I₁ and I₂.

The magnetic field produced by wire 1 at the position of wire 2 is:

B₁ = μ₀ I₁ / (2πd)

The magnetic force on a length L of wire 2 is F = I₂LB₁, because the wire is perpendicular to the magnetic field.

F = μ₀ I₁ I₂ L / (2πd)
F / L = μ₀ I₁ I₂ / (2πd)

Here μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ N A⁻² in vacuum. In a medium, replace μ₀ with its permeability μ for the ideal homogeneous-medium expression.

3. What changes the force?

ChangeEffect on F/L
Double I₁ only2 times
Double both currents4 times
Double separation dHalf
Reduce separation to d/33 times
Reverse one currentAttraction ↔ repulsion
The magnitude depends on the product I₁I₂ and is inversely proportional to separation d.

4. Worked numerical example

Two long parallel wires carry 2 A and 3 A. Their separation is 0.5 m. Find the force per unit length in vacuum.

Step 1: Write the formula.

F/L = μ₀ I₁ I₂ / (2πd)

Step 2: Substitute values.

F/L = (4π × 10⁻⁷ × 2 × 3) / (2π × 0.5)

Answer:

F/L = 2.4 × 10⁻⁶ N m⁻¹

5. Exam checkpoints

  • Force is attractive for currents in the same direction.
  • Force is repulsive for currents in opposite directions.
  • For long wires, force per unit length is independent of the chosen segment length.
  • SI unit of force per unit length is N m⁻¹.
  • The old SI definition of ampere used the force between parallel current-carrying conductors; the current SI definition is based on the elementary charge.
