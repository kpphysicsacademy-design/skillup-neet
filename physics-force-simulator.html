How is it calculated?

Fnet = Fapplied − Ffriction = 20 − 5 = 15 N
a = Fnet / m = 15 / 5 = 3 m/s²
W = mg = 5 × 9.8 = 49 N

*Assuming a horizontal surface, no vertical acceleration and no additional vertical forces. Friction is modelled as a constant opposing force and cannot exceed the applied force in this simplified example.

The applied force is greater than friction, so the block accelerates to the right.
