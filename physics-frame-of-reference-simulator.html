🚆 Simulator 1: Passenger in a moving train

Set the train velocity and the passenger's walking velocity relative to the train. Positive means forward/east; negative means backward/west.

Ground velocity = 22 m/s (positive direction)

Use a consistent positive direction for both velocities.

↔️ Simulator 2: Relative velocity between two objects

Enter both velocities along the same straight line. The result is the velocity of object A as seen from object B.

Velocity of A relative to B = 30 m/s

A positive result means A moves in the chosen positive direction relative to B; a negative result means the opposite.
