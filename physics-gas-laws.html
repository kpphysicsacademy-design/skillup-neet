💨 MOLECULAR NATURE OF MATTER

Gas Laws

Understand how pressure, volume, temperature and amount of gas are related. Use absolute temperature in gas-law calculations.

PV = nRT

📚 Core Gas Laws

Boyle’s Law
PV = constant
At constant temperature and amount of gas, pressure is inversely proportional to volume.
Charles’s Law
V/T = constant
At constant pressure and amount of gas, volume is directly proportional to absolute temperature.
Gay-Lussac’s Law
P/T = constant
At constant volume and amount of gas, pressure is directly proportional to absolute temperature.
Avogadro’s Law
V/n = constant
At constant pressure and temperature, volume is directly proportional to the amount of gas.
Combined Gas Law
P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂
For a fixed amount of gas, the pressure, volume and absolute temperature are related by this expression.

🧠 Quick Rules

Temperature
Convert Celsius to Kelvin before using gas-law equations: T(K) = t(°C) + 273.15.
Ideal-gas equation
PV = nRT
P is pressure, V is volume, n is amount in moles, R is the universal gas constant and T is absolute temperature.
Ideal behaviour
Real gases approach ideal behaviour more closely at relatively high temperature and low pressure.
