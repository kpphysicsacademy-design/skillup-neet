📘 Inclined Plane · Learn

Practice inclined plane physics with 30 interactive questions and explanations.

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. For a block on a smooth incline at angle θ to the horizontal, the component of weight down the plane is:

Key point: Resolving weight along the incline gives mg sin θ down the plane.

Answer: mg sin θ

2. For a block on an incline at angle θ, with no other perpendicular forces, the normal reaction is:

Key point: The component of weight perpendicular to the plane is mg cos θ, balanced by the normal reaction.

Answer: mg cos θ

3. A block on a smooth incline accelerates down the plane at:

Key point: Along the plane, mg sin θ = ma, so a = g sin θ.

Answer: g sin θ

4. At θ = 0°, a smooth plane is:

Key point: An incline at zero degrees to the horizontal is a horizontal surface.

Answer: Horizontal

5. At θ = 90°, the plane is:

Key point: A plane making 90° with the horizontal is vertical.

Answer: Vertical
