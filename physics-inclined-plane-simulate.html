Model equations

W = mg
N = mg cos θ
W∥ = mg sin θ
fₖ = μₖN
a = g(sin θ − μₖ cos θ)

Assumption: the block is already moving down the plane. If friction is enough to prevent motion from rest, static friction must be considered instead. Uses g = 9.8 m/s².
