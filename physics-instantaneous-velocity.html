Instantaneous Velocity

A short lesson about velocity at one moment.

📘 Learn🎮 Simulator🧠 Practice

1. Meaning

Instantaneous velocity is the velocity of an object at a particular instant.

v = dx / dt

It is the rate of change of position with time. SI unit: m/s. It is a vector.

2. Position–time graph

Instantaneous velocity equals the slope of the tangent to the position–time graph at that instant.

v = slope = dx / dt

3. Simple example

Position: x = 3t²
Velocity: v = dx/dt = 6t
At t = 2 s: v = 6 × 2 = 12 m/s.

Remember

• Average velocity is measured over a time interval.
• Instantaneous velocity is measured at one moment.
• Negative velocity shows motion in the negative direction.
• Speed is the magnitude of velocity.
