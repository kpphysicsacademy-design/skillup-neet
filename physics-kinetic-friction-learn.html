Kinetic Friction

Kinetic friction acts when two surfaces slide relative to each other. It acts parallel to the contact surfaces and opposes relative sliding.

fₖ = μₖN

1. Coefficient of kinetic friction

μₖ is the coefficient of kinetic friction. It depends on the nature and condition of the contacting surfaces and is dimensionless.

2. Direction

Kinetic friction acts opposite to the relative sliding velocity at the contact. Determine the direction of motion before drawing the friction arrow.

3. Horizontal surface

When no other vertical forces act, N = mg. Therefore:

fₖ = μₖmg

4. Inclined plane

For a block sliding on an incline at angle θ, the normal reaction is N = mg cos θ.

fₖ = μₖmg cos θ

If the block slides down the plane, kinetic friction acts up the plane.

5. Effects and applications

  • Friction slows sliding objects.
  • Brakes use friction to reduce vehicle speed.
  • Lubricants reduce friction and wear in machinery.

Remember

Kinetic friction applies during sliding. Static friction applies when surfaces do not slide relative to each other.

Worked example

A 5 kg block slides on a horizontal floor. If μₖ = 0.2 and g = 10 m/s², calculate kinetic friction.

N = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N
fₖ = μₖN = 0.2 × 50 = 10 N

Friction acts opposite to the direction of sliding.
