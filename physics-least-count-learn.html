📘 Least Count · Learn

SkillUp Physics Easy Mode: Least Count quiz Explore Least Count Quiz | Physics in Physics, understand the key relationships and practise focused questions

Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. What is the least count of an instrument?

Key point: Least count is the smallest measurement increment an instrument can resolve.

Answer: The smallest value it can measure directly

2. A ruler has markings every 1 mm. Its least count is:

Key point: The smallest division is 1 mm.

Answer: 1 mm

3. A scale has 10 equal divisions in 1 cm. What is its least count?

Key point: 1 cm divided by 10 equals 0.1 cm, or 1 mm.

Answer: 1 mm

4. Which instrument usually has a smaller least count for length?

Key point: Vernier calipers can resolve smaller lengths than coarse scales.

Answer: Vernier calipers

5. A screw gauge has pitch 1 mm and 100 circular divisions. Its least count is:

Key point: Least count = pitch ÷ circular scale divisions = 1/100 mm = 0.01 mm.

Answer: 0.01 mm
