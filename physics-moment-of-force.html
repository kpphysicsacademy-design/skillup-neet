ROTATIONAL MOTION • CONCEPT GUIDE

Moment of Force

Learn how a force turns an object about a pivot, how the perpendicular moment arm works, and how to solve numerical problems.

Core conceptFormula + examplesExam revision
01 • MEANING

What is moment of force?

The moment of a force about a point is the turning effect produced by that force about the point or axis. It depends on the magnitude of force and the perpendicular distance from the pivot to the force's line of action.

Everyday example: A door is easier to open when you push at its handle rather than close to its hinges. The handle gives a larger moment arm.

A longer perpendicular distance from the pivot produces a greater moment for the same force.

02 • FORMULA

Moment of force formula

M = F × d⊥

F is the applied force in newtons and d⊥ is the shortest perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force.

M = rF sin θ
  • r — distance from pivot to the point of application (m)
  • θ — angle between position vector r and force F
  • SI unit — newton metre (N m)
  • Dimensions — ML²T⁻²
Only the perpendicular component F sin θ produces a moment. The component parallel to r produces no turning effect about the pivot.
03 • ANGLE & LEVER ARM

Effect of the angle

For fixed F and r, the moment depends on sin θ.

0°M = 0Force along radius
30°M = ½rFHalf maximum
90°M = rFMaximum moment
180°M = 0Force opposite radius

The moment is maximum when the force is perpendicular to the position vector and zero when the force acts along the position vector.

04 • DIRECTION & SIGN

Clockwise and anticlockwise moments

For a body in the plane of the page, use the right-hand rule to identify the moment direction. A common sign convention is:

↶
AnticlockwisePositive (+)
↷
ClockwiseNegative (−)

The convention may be reversed, but keep it consistent when summing moments.

05 • PRINCIPLE OF MOMENTS

Rotational equilibrium

When a body is in rotational equilibrium, the algebraic sum of all moments about a point is zero.

ΣM = 0

Therefore, total clockwise moments equal total anticlockwise moments about the same pivot.

Example: A 12 N m clockwise moment is balanced by a 12 N m anticlockwise moment. Net moment = 0.

For a rigid body rotating about a fixed axis, the net moment is related to angular acceleration by:

Mnet = Iα

Zero net moment means zero angular acceleration; the body may still rotate at constant angular velocity.

06 • COUPLE

Moment of a couple

A couple is formed by two equal, opposite and parallel forces acting along different lines. The net force is zero, but the forces produce a pure turning effect.

Mcouple = Fd

Here d is the perpendicular separation between the two lines of action. Examples include turning a steering wheel or twisting a jar lid with both hands.

07 • SOLVED NUMERICALS

Step-by-step examples

Example 1 — Perpendicular force

A 5 N force acts perpendicular to a lever at 2 m from the pivot. Find its moment.

  1. M = Fd = 5 × 2.
  2. Moment = 10 N m.
Example 2 — Inclined force

A 20 N force acts at 30° to a 0.5 m position vector. Find the moment.

  1. M = rF sin θ.
  2. M = 0.5 × 20 × sin 30°.
  3. Moment = 5 N m.
Example 3 — Find the perpendicular distance

A 30 N perpendicular force produces a moment of 15 N m. Find d.

  1. d = M/F = 15/30.
  2. d = 0.5 m.
08 • EXAM CHECKPOINT

Common mistakes

  • Using the distance along the force direction instead of the perpendicular distance.
  • Using cos θ instead of sin θ when θ is measured between r and F.
  • Forgetting to assign signs to clockwise and anticlockwise moments.
  • Assuming zero net moment means the body cannot rotate.
  • Confusing moment of force with work. Both may use N m, but they represent different quantities.
Remember: Moment = force × perpendicular distance from pivot to the line of action.
CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Quick concept checks

1. A 12 N force acts with a perpendicular moment arm of 0.25 m. Find its moment.

Reveal answer

3 N m. M = 12 × 0.25.

2. What happens to the moment if the force is doubled while the moment arm stays fixed?

Reveal answer

It doubles. Moment is directly proportional to force.

3. When is the moment of a force zero about a pivot?

Reveal answer

When the line of action passes through the pivot. The perpendicular distance is zero.
