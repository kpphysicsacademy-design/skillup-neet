Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. What does moment of inertia describe?

Key point: Moment of inertia measures how strongly a body's mass distribution resists angular acceleration about an axis.

Answer: Resistance to angular acceleration

2. For a point mass m at perpendicular distance r from an axis, I equals:

Key point: For a point particle, moment of inertia is I = mr².

Answer: mr²

3. What is the SI unit of moment of inertia?

Key point: Mass times distance squared gives the SI unit kg m².

Answer: kg m²

4. Which change increases a point mass's moment of inertia most directly?

Key point: Moment of inertia depends on r², so increasing distance increases I.

Answer: Moving it farther from the axis

5. Two equal masses are at distances r and 2r from an axis. The second mass has moment of inertia:

Key point: Since I = mr², doubling r makes the moment of inertia four times larger.

Answer: Four times as large
