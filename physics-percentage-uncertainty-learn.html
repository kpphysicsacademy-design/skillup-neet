Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. What is percentage uncertainty?

Key point: Percentage uncertainty = (absolute uncertainty ÷ measured value) × 100%.

Answer: Absolute uncertainty divided by measured value, multiplied by 100%

2. A length is (20.0 ± 0.2) cm. What is its percentage uncertainty?

Key point: (0.2 ÷ 20.0) × 100% = 1%.

Answer: 1%

3. A mass is 50 g with an absolute uncertainty of 1 g. Find the percentage uncertainty.

Key point: (1 ÷ 50) × 100% = 2%.

Answer: 2%

4. A measured time is 4.0 s ± 0.1 s. What is the percentage uncertainty?

Key point: (0.1 ÷ 4.0) × 100% = 2.5%.

Answer: 2.5%

5. If absolute uncertainty stays the same, what happens to percentage uncertainty when the measured value increases?

Key point: The same absolute uncertainty is a smaller fraction of a larger measured value.

Answer: It decreases
