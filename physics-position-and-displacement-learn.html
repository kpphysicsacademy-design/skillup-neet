Study notes below are drawn from this topic's existing question bank and explanations.

1. What does position describe?

Key point: Position gives an object's location relative to an origin in a reference frame.

Answer: Where an object is relative to a chosen origin

2. What is displacement?

Key point: Displacement is final position minus initial position and includes direction.

Answer: Change in position from initial to final point

3. A student moves from x = 2 m to x = 8 m. Displacement is:

Key point: Displacement = 8 − 2 = +6 m.

Answer: +6 m

4. A person moves from x = 9 m to x = 4 m. Displacement is:

Key point: Displacement = 4 − 9 = −5 m.

Answer: −5 m

5. Which quantity is a vector?

Key point: Displacement has magnitude and direction.

Answer: Displacement
