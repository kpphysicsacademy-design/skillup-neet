🛞 Rolling Motion

Understand how an object moves forward while rotating about its centre.

vcm = Rω

Rolling motion combines translation of the centre of mass with rotation about the centre of mass.

1. Pure rolling (without slipping)

For a wheel rolling on a stationary surface without slipping, the contact point is instantaneously at rest relative to the ground.

vcm = Rω

Here v is centre-of-mass speed, R is radius and ω is angular speed in rad/s.

Remember: Top point speed = 2v; centre speed = v; contact point speed = 0 (instantaneously).

2. Velocities around a rolling wheel

Top point
2v

Translation and rotation add.

Contact point
0

The velocity contributions cancel.

These values apply to pure rolling without slipping.

3. Acceleration relation

acm = Rα

The magnitude of linear acceleration equals radius multiplied by angular acceleration. Apply a consistent sign convention for direction.

4. Kinetic energy

K = ½Mv² + ½Icmω²

Total kinetic energy is translational plus rotational energy. Using v = Rω:

K = ½Mv² (1 + Icm / MR²)

5. Worked example

A wheel of radius 0.4 m rolls without slipping at 2 m/s. Find its angular speed.

ω = v/R = 2/0.4 = 5 rad/s.

6. Quick revision

  • Pure rolling: v = Rω.
  • Top point speed: 2v.
  • Contact point speed: zero instantaneously.
  • Rolling kinetic energy has translational and rotational parts.
  • Slipping means the no-slip condition is not satisfied.
