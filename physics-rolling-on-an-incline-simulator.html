What should you notice?

A larger moment of inertia ratio k = I/(mR²) gives a smaller acceleration. A solid sphere accelerates faster than a solid cylinder, and a thin hoop is slowest among these choices.

Ideal model: released from rest, pure rolling, no energy losses, g = 9.8 m/s². Static friction acts up the slope and its magnitude is fₛ = [k/(1+k)]mg sinθ. For a real surface, sufficient static friction is required to prevent slipping.
