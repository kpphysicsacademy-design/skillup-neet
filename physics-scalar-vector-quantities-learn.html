📌 Quick Cheat Sheet

Scalar: magnitude only. Examples: mass, time, temperature, distance, speed, energy.

Vector: magnitude and direction; follows vector addition. Examples: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, momentum.

Components: Aₓ = A cos θ, Aᵧ = A sin θ; |A| = √(Aₓ² + Aᵧ²).

Vector addition: Rₓ = Aₓ + Bₓ, Rᵧ = Aᵧ + Bᵧ; |R| = √(Rₓ² + Rᵧ²).

Example: 3 m east + 4 m north = 5 m at 53.1° north of east.

NEET tip: distance ≥ |displacement|. A negative component indicates direction, not negative magnitude.
