📘 Quick Learn

Shear modulus (G), also called modulus of rigidity, measures a material's resistance to deformation caused by a tangential force.

Shear stress = F/A. Shear strain = Δx/L. Therefore G = FL/(AΔx). Its SI unit is pascal (Pa), and its dimensions are [M L⁻¹ T⁻²].
