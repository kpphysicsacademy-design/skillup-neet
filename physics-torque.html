ROTATIONAL MOTION • CONCEPT GUIDE

Torque

Discover how a force produces rotation, why a longer handle makes turning easier, and how to calculate the turning effect.

Core conceptFormula + diagramsWorked examples
01 • MEANING

What is torque?

Torque is the turning effect of a force about a pivot or axis. A force can cause rotation only if its line of action has a non-zero perpendicular distance from the axis.

Think of a door: Pushing near the handle turns it more easily than pushing close to the hinges because the handle is farther from the pivot.

A larger perpendicular distance from the pivot gives a larger torque for the same force.

02 • FORMULA

Magnitude of torque

τ = rF sin θ

Where:

  • τ (tau) — torque magnitude, measured in N m
  • r — distance from the pivot to the point of application (m)
  • F — applied force (N)
  • θ — smaller angle between the position vector r and force F
The perpendicular component of force is F sin θ. Therefore, torque is also τ = r × F⊥.
τ = F × d⊥

Here d⊥ is the perpendicular (moment-arm) distance from the pivot to the force's line of action.

03 • ANGLE MATTERS

How the angle changes torque

For fixed force and distance, torque depends on sin θ.

0°τ = 0Force along the radius
30°τ = ½ rFSmaller turning effect
90°τ = rFMaximum torque
180°τ = 0Force points along radius

Torque is maximum when force is perpendicular to the position vector and zero when the force is parallel or antiparallel to it.

04 • DIRECTION

Clockwise and anticlockwise torque

Torque is a vector. For rotation in the plane of the page, the right-hand rule gives its direction perpendicular to the page.

↶
AnticlockwisePositive (+) by the usual convention
↷
ClockwiseNegative (−) by the usual convention

The sign convention can be chosen differently, but use one convention consistently throughout a problem.

05 • MULTIPLE FORCES

Net torque and rotational equilibrium

When several forces act on a body, calculate each torque about the same pivot and add them algebraically:

τnet = Στ

For a rigid body about a fixed axis:

τnet = Iα
  • If net torque is zero, angular acceleration is zero.
  • The body may remain at rest or continue rotating with constant angular velocity.
  • For complete static equilibrium, both net force and net torque must be zero.
Balance example: If 12 N m acts anticlockwise and 12 N m acts clockwise, the net torque is zero.
06 • COUPLE

Moment of a couple

A couple consists of two equal, opposite and parallel forces whose lines of action are separated. The resultant force is zero, but the pair produces rotation.

τcouple = Fd

d is the perpendicular distance between the two lines of action. Examples include turning a steering wheel with both hands or twisting a jar lid.

07 • SOLVED EXAMPLES

Apply the formula

Example 1 — Perpendicular force

A 10 N force acts perpendicular to a spanner 0.5 m from the nut. Find the torque.

  1. Given: F = 10 N, r = 0.5 m, θ = 90°.
  2. τ = rF sin θ.
  3. τ = 0.5 × 10 × 1 = 5 N m.
Example 2 — Inclined force

A 20 N force acts at 30° to a 2 m position vector. Find the torque magnitude.

  1. Given: F = 20 N, r = 2 m, θ = 30°.
  2. τ = 2 × 20 × sin 30°.
  3. τ = 40 × 0.5 = 20 N m.
Example 3 — Find the force

A perpendicular force produces 15 N m torque at a distance of 0.3 m. Find the force.

  1. τ = rF, so F = τ/r.
  2. F = 15/0.3 = 50 N.
08 • EXAM CHECKPOINT

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using cos θ instead of sin θ when θ is measured between r and F.
  • Using the full distance instead of the perpendicular moment arm.
  • Forgetting the sign of clockwise and anticlockwise torques.
  • Assuming zero net torque means the object cannot rotate. It can rotate at constant angular velocity.
  • Confusing torque with work: both can be expressed using N m, but they are different physical quantities.
Remember: Torque depends on force, distance from the pivot, and the angle between them.
CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Quick concept checks

1. A 15 N force acts perpendicular to a 0.4 m wrench. What is the torque?

Reveal answer

6 N m. τ = rF = 0.4 × 15.

2. What happens to torque if the force is doubled while r and θ stay constant?

Reveal answer

It doubles. Torque is directly proportional to force.

3. A force acts directly along the position vector. What is its torque about the pivot?

Reveal answer

Zero. The angle is 0° or 180°, so sin θ = 0.
