Young’s Modulus

Learn stiffness through stress, strain, the Young’s modulus formula and NEET-style practice.

Y = σ / ε = FL / (AΔL)
σ = F/A • Stressε = ΔL/L • StrainSI unit: PaHigh Y → more stiff
🎯 NEET Quick Learn

Young’s modulus is the ratio of longitudinal stress to longitudinal strain within the elastic region. Since strain is dimensionless, Y has the same unit as stress: N m⁻² or pascal.

Remember: Y belongs to the material; the extension of a particular wire also depends on its length and cross-sectional area.
