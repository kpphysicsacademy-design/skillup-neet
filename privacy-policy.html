Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or use SkillUp NEET and its learning, practice and quiz features.

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Publisher review required: This policy must be checked against the live website/app and every enabled SDK before it is submitted to Google Play. Remove or amend statements that do not describe the actual product. Add the publisher's monitored privacy contact details before publication.

1. Scope

SkillUp NEET is an educational platform intended to help learners study subjects, practise questions and review quiz results. This policy applies to the SkillUp NEET website and related app features that link to this policy.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use and the services enabled in the deployed version, SkillUp NEET may process:

  • Information you provide: details you submit through account, feedback, support or other forms, if those features are available.
  • Learning activity: selected answers, quiz scores, progress or preferences, if these are saved by the product.
  • Technical information: browser or device information, diagnostic events, and basic usage data that may be recorded by hosting, security or analytics services.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information in free-text fields unless a feature specifically requests it and explains why.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used, where applicable, to deliver learning and quiz features, remember progress or preferences, respond to support requests, maintain security, diagnose errors, improve performance, and meet legal obligations.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website or app may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to remember settings, maintain sessions, support core functionality, or measure performance if those functions are enabled. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling storage may affect some features.

5. Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by providers used to host, secure, maintain or operate the product, but only where those providers are configured in the deployed service. Such providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer permitted by law.

SkillUp NEET should not claim that it uses or does not use a particular analytics, advertising, authentication or database provider unless that has been verified against the production build and website.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal, security and operational requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

SkillUp NEET may be used by students, including younger learners. The publisher must configure the app's target audience and comply with applicable child-safety and family policies. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately should contact the publisher using the official contact method published on the SkillUp website so the request can be reviewed.

8. Your choices and data requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You can also stop using the service and manage relevant browser permissions. Requests should be made through the official contact method published on the SkillUp website. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

If the app allows users to create accounts, the publisher must provide an account-deletion process that meets Google Play requirements and ensure this policy accurately explains how deletion requests work.

9. Third-party links

The service may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and SkillUp NEET is not responsible for their independent practices.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the official contact method published on skillupedu.in. The publisher should ensure that a monitored contact address or contact form is available and clearly identify the responsible organisation or person before this policy is submitted for app review.
