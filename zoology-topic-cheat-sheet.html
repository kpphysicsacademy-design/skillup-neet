🌱 What makes something living?

Living organisms are organised cellular systems that carry out metabolism and sense and respond to changes in their surroundings. No single outward movement or increase in size is enough to identify life.

⚙️
Metabolism

The sum total of all chemical reactions in a living system. Includes anabolism and catabolism.

🧫
Cellular organisation

All living organisms are made of cells. A single cell can perform essential life functions in unicellular organisms.

🌿
Growth

Biological growth involves an increase in cell number and/or cell mass.

👀
Consciousness

Ability to sense environmental changes and respond to stimuli.

🔍 Important NCERT distinctions

ConceptRemember this
GrowthLiving growth is internal and linked to cell division or enlargement. Crystals can also grow by external deposition.
ReproductionImportant for continuity of a species, but not an essential feature of every individual. Mules and worker bees are examples of sterile individuals.
MetabolismIncludes anabolic (building) and catabolic (breaking down) reactions. It is a defining feature of living systems.
ResponsePlants bending towards light and bacteria moving towards nutrients are responses to stimuli.
DormancyA dormant seed is living; it can resume metabolic activity when conditions become suitable.

⚡ Fast recall

Anabolism = builds complex molecules. Catabolism = breaks molecules down. Metabolism = both together.

Do not confuse: movement, increase in size, or reproduction alone cannot be used as the sole test of life.
