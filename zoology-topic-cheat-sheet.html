🌱 1. What makes something living?

Living organisms are cellular systems that show metabolism and consciousness — the ability to sense their surroundings and respond. They are also self-regulating, self-replicating and interactive systems. No single outward movement, increase in size or reproduction alone is enough to define life.

⚙️
Metabolism

The sum total of all chemical reactions in a living system. Includes anabolism and catabolism.

🧫
Cellular organisation

All living organisms are made of cells. A single cell can perform essential life functions in unicellular organisms.

🌿
Growth

Growth is an increase in mass and/or number of cells. In living organisms it occurs from within, commonly through cell division; non-living things such as crystals can increase in size by surface deposition.

👀
Consciousness

The ability to sense the environment and mount a suitable response to stimuli. This is a defining property of living organisms.

🔍 2. Important NCERT distinctions

ConceptRemember this
GrowthLiving growth is from within, usually through cell division. Non-living objects such as mountains and crystals may increase in size by external accumulation. Therefore, growth alone is not a defining feature.
ReproductionHelps continuity of a species, but is not a defining feature of every living individual. Mules and sterile worker bees do not reproduce. In unicellular organisms, cell division may represent both growth and reproduction.
MetabolismThe sum total of all chemical reactions in an organism: anabolism (building) + catabolism (breaking down). It is a defining feature of living organisms. An isolated reaction in a test tube is not itself a living organism.
Consciousness / responseAbility to sense surroundings and respond. Examples: a plant shoot bends towards light; bacteria move towards nutrients.
Cellular organisationLiving organisms have an organised cellular structure. Cellular organisation is a defining feature of life.

⚡ 3. Fast recall

Anabolism = builds complex molecules. Catabolism = breaks molecules down. Metabolism = anabolism + catabolism.

Three defining features to remember: metabolism, cellular organisation and consciousness.

Not defining on their own: growth and reproduction. Movement alone is not proof of life.
